## SELLER shall be responsible for, indemnify, defend and hold the BUYER Group harmless from and against all TAXATION assessed or levied against or on account of wages, salaries or other emoluments or deemed benefits paid to SELLER GROUP’s PERSONNEL or any other person employed by any member of the SELLER Group in connection with the CONTRACT, and any claims in connection with such TAXATION obligations. Without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing, the SELLER shall be responsible for compliance with all statutory obligations to make deduction on account of and to remit the required amounts to any appropriate government authority including, but not limited to income tax, pay as you earn, national insurance, employee taxes, charges, social security costs, levies and contributions whether or not they are measured by the wages, salaries or other remuneration or benefits paid to persons employed by the SELLER, or persons providing services in connection with the CONTRACT and the imposition of a similar obligation upon all members of the SELLER or any other persons employed by them or providing services to them in connection with the CONTRACT. 卖方应负责、赔偿、保护买方集团，使其免受因支付给卖方集团人员或卖方集团任何成员雇佣的与合同相关的任何其他人员的工资、薪金或其他报酬或视为福利而评估或征收的所有税款，以及与此类纳税义务相关的任何索赔。在不影响上述一般性的情况下，卖方应负责遵守所有法定义务，扣除所需金额，并将所需金额汇至任何适当的政府机构，包括但不限于所得税、收入随付、国家保险、员工税、费用、社会保障成本、征税和缴款，无论其是否通过支付给卖方雇佣的人员或提供合同相关服务的人员的工资、薪金或其他报酬或福利来衡量，以及对卖方所有成员或其雇佣的任何其他人员或向其提供合同相关服务的人员施加类似义务。
