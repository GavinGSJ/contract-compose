## The SELLER shall allow the BUYER to enter his factory and verify the above production progress in detail. Both parties shall sign the verified production progress report as the basis of termination settlement.

卖方应允许买方进驻其工厂，对以上生产进度进行详细核实，双方须对核实后的生产进度报告进行签字，作为合同终止结算的依据。
