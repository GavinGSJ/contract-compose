## SELLER waives any right of recourse against, and shall be liable for and shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless BUYER GROUP from and against any and all INDEMNIFIED COSTS of whatever nature and howsoever caused, in respect of, or arising out of:

卖方放弃对买方集团的任何追索权，并应对买方集团因以下原因而产生的任何和所有赔偿费用负责，并应保护买方集团，使其免受损害：

- injury, illness or death of any PERSONNEL of SELLER GROUP; and/or卖方集团任何人员的受伤、疾病或死亡；和/或

- loss of, or damage to the GOODS (up to the point when the CONTRACT GOODS has been delivered to BUYER) 货物损失或损坏（截至合同货物交付给买方之日）

- loss of, or damage to any property, owned, hired or leased, of any member of SELLER GROUP; and/or卖方集团任何成员拥有、租用或租赁的任何财产的损失或损坏；和/或

- loss of, or damage to the BUYER provided items and/or any other property, owned, hired or leased, of any member of BUYER GROUP (other than the GOODS) while under the care, custody, control, protection and/or preservation of any member of SELLER GROUP. 在卖方集团任何成员的照管、保管、控制、保护和/或保存下，买方集团任何成员（货物除外）拥有、租用或租赁的买方提供的物品和/或任何其他财产的损失或损坏。
