## If the CONTRACT is terminated for reasons attributable to the BUYER, the BUYER, as the sole agreed remedy (whether in contract, tort or otherwise) shall pay to the SELLER the price corresponding to the CONTRACT GOODS actually delivered as at the date of termination, in accordance with the verified status of progress and the corresponding price, and provided that such goods are in the possession of the BUYER after payment of the price. If both PARTIES agree that the title to the corresponding goods remains with the SELLER, the corresponding value of the goods shall be deducted in the termination settlement. Title to the GOODS furnished by SELLER hereunder shall be transferred to BUYER upon payment of the GOODS; be it half-completed or any portion of sub-components, for which orders already placed by SELLER, under the agreed delivery terms of the CONTRACT.

如因买方原因终止合同，作为唯一商定的补救措施（无论是在合同、侵权或其他方面），买方应根据核实的进度状态及对应价格，向卖方支付截至终止之日已经实际交付的货物所对应的价款，前提是此类货物在支付价格后归买方所有。或者如果双方达成一致此类货物所有权仍归卖方所有，其对应的货值在合同终止结算中进行抵扣。卖方根据本合同提供的货物的所有权应在支付货物款项后转移给买方；无论是半成品还是卖方已根据本合同商定的交付条款下订单的任何部分子订单。
