## The erection, test-run, performance test of the CONTRACT GOODS shall be carried out by BUYER following the technical instructions given by SELLER’s representative. Each party shall authorize one representative to deal with all technical matters in connection with the acceptance of the CONTRACT GOODS during the site erection, installation, site acceptance test (SAT) up to the commissioning and start-up of the CONTRACT GOODS. The representatives of both PARTIES shall make detailed plan through friendly discussion. Any disagreement shall be settled through friendly negotiation without prejudice to the provisions of this CONTRACT.

合同货物的安装，试运行和性能测试应由买方按照卖方代表的技术指导下进行。从到现场、安装、现场验收试验（SAT）直到合同货物调试和启动期间，双方应授权一名代表在现场处理与合同货物验收有关的所有的技术问题。双方代表应通过友好协商做出详细安排。双方代表的任何不一致意见，应在合同项下条款范围内友好协商解决。

During the site installation, SAT, commissioning and start-up as well as site training, SELLER shall dispatch competent technical PERSONNEL at his own costs for site TECHNICAL SERVICE which have been detailed in the TECHNICAL AGREEMENT. The number of days of free site services has been detailed out in ATTACHMENT B. Beyond that, BUYER shall compensate SELLER at the service rates given in ATTACHMENT B. SELLER’s PERSONNEL shall be authorized to give technical instructions and to solve all technical problems within the scope of the CONTRACT.

在现场组装、现场验收试验、试运行、启动和培训期间，卖方应自费派遣合格技术人员进行技术服务，详见技术协议。免费现场服务的天数已在附件B中详细说明。除此之外，买方应按附件B中规定的服务费率对卖方进行补偿。卖方技术人员有权应提供技术指导，并解决所有合同范围内的技术问题。
