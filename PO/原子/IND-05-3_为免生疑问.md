### For the avoidance of doubt, no members of either PARTY’s GROUP shall be considered as third party, and, for purposes of SELLER’s indemnity obligation in this Article, the SELLER GROUP’s worksite(s) or any property or facilities thereon shall be considered SELLER GROUP’s property.

为免生疑问，任何一方集团的任何成员均不得被视为第三方，并且，就本条中卖方的赔偿义务而言，卖方集团的工地或其上的任何财产或设施均应被视为卖方集团的财产。
