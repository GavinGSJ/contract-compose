## The warranty period applicable to any SUPPLY to rectify defective equipment, parts and workmanship shall be for a further 12 months from the date of repair or replacement or until the end of the warranty period stated in Article {{ref:QUA-03}}, whichever is longer.

适用于修复缺陷设备、部件和工艺的任何供货的质保期应从维修或更换之日起再延长12个月，或至第{{ref:QUA-03}}条规定的质保期结束为止，以较长者为准。

If it is necessary for any defective part(s) of the CONTRACT GOODS to be removed from the PROJECT for repair or replacement, the SELLER shall be liable for the BUYER’s reasonable costs of removing the defective part(s) and re-installing repaired or replacement part(s). Furthermore, where repair or replacement involves the return of defective part(s) to the SELLER’s premises or any to other place for repair, the carriage and insurance costs of shipment to the relevant place of repair and returning repaired or replacement part(s) duties to the BUYER, including customs clearance fees, customs duties, shall be borne by the SELLER. All other direct costs suffered by BUYER, including but not limited to the standby cost, financial damage during the unavailability of SUPPLY shall be borne by the SELLER, too.

如果有缺陷的货物或者部件因维修或者更换需要自项目装置内拆除，卖方应支付给买方因拆除缺陷货物或者部件以及重新安装维修或者更换部件所产生的合理费用。此外，如果维修或者更换涉及将缺陷货物或者部件返回卖方工厂或其他指定地点进行的，运输缺陷货物或部件以及返回相应货物或部件而对买方所产生的运输及保险费用、税费等（包括清关费用、关税）应由卖方承担。在货物无法使用这段期间而导致买方的直接损失，包括但不限于窝工费，财务损失等将由卖方承担。
