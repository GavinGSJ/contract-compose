## The BUYER is entitled to terminate this CONTRACT at any time in accordance with the specific conditions of the OWNER/end user and the PROJECT. 买方有权根据业主/最终用户和项目的具体情况随时终止本合同。

SELLER’s obligation under this CONTRACT may be terminated by BUYER in accordance with this Article in part, if BUYER shall so elect, under given circumstances. Upon receipt of the Notice of Termination specifying the extent to which performance under the CONTRACT is terminated and the date upon which such termination becomes effective, SELLER shall: 在特定情况下，如买方做出选择，买方可根据本条部分终止卖方在本合同项下的义务。卖方在收到注明终止履行范围及终止生效日期的终止通知后，应：

- Immediately discontinue work on the date and to the extent specified in the notice; 在通知规定的日期和范围内立即停止工作；

- Place no further SUBCONTRACT for materials required for the provision of the SUPPLY; 不再为完成供货所需的材料下任何分订单；

- Promptly make every reasonable effort to either obtain cancellation, on the terms satisfactory to the BUYER for all orders to sub suppliers.迅速做出一切合理努力，按照买方满意的条款，取消向分包商发出的所有订单。

SELLER shall submit, within (7) working days, a detailed production status report listing the following, with the effect of such termination:

卖方应在（7）个工作日内提交一份详细的生产状况报告，说明终止合同的影响，包括但不限于以下文件：

- list of finished goods and prices已完成成品清单及价格

- list of semi-finished products and prices半成品清单及价格

- raw materials that have not been started未开工生产的原材料

- corresponding photographs of the above-listed items以上所列物资的对应照片
