## All payment made by the PARTIES under this CONTRACT shall be made through respective banks by telegraphic transfer (T/T) or Letter of Credit (L/C). Unless otherwise specified in the CONTRACT, all documents of payment made by the PARTIES shall be transferred directly. Any bank issuing an L/C payment on behalf of SELLER shall be subject to BUYER’s approval. Such account shall be in SELLER’s name and shall be maintained either in the country of performance of the SUPPLY or in SELLER’s domicile.

双方在本合同下所有付款都应通过各自的银行电汇或信用证的方式进行支付，除非合同另有规定，双方所有的支付相关文件都应直接沟通传递。代表卖方开具信用证付款的任何银行均应获得买方的批准。该账户名应与本合同卖方名称保持一致，并在供货实施地国家或卖方所在地注册。
