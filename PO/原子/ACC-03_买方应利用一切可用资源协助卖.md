## BUYER shall use all available resources to assist SELLER with such activities. After all necessary modifications and/or repair and/or replacement have been completed by SELLER, a performance test / SAT shall be carried out again as soon as possible in accordance with TECHNICAL AGREEMENT.

买方应利用一切可用资源协助卖方进行这些行动。在卖方进行必要的更正、修理、或更换后，应根据合同技术协议的要求尽快进行性能试验/现场验收试验。
