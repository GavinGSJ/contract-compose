## If SELLER or its SUB-SUPPLIER’s PERSONNEL are unable to present for site TECHNICAL SERVICE because of Force Majeure, BUYER has the right to operate, commission and/or repair the CONTRACT GOODS in accordance with the SELLER’s manual or instructions, and such performance shall not affect the SELLER’s warranty responsibility and shall not relieve SELLER from any of his obligations under this CONTRACT.

若需要卖方或者其分包商厂家的人员因不可抗力等原因无法到现场时，买方有权按照卖方的操作手册或说明书对合同货物进行操作、调试和/或维修，但不免除卖方在合同项下的任何责任。
