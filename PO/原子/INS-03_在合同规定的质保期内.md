## Should the quality of the delivered GOODS not be in conformity with the CONTRACT or not meeting the specifications of the TECHNICAL AGREEMENT, or should such GOODS prove to be defective within the warranty period stipulated in the CONTRACT for any reason, including latent defect or the use of unsuitable materials, the BUYER shall arrange for a joint investigation with SELLER. If SELLER is found to be responsible, all related costs shall be borne by SELLER including but not limited to: a) the cost of dispatch for SELLER representative for joint inspection b) dismantling and handling costs of the said GOODS, if applicable c) return of defective CONTRACT GOODS to SELLER’s premise for repair or replacement, d) cost for repair or replacement, e) all relevant tests and re-certification costs, f) cost for delivery of repaired GOODS or replacement g) handling and installation cost h) additional site acceptance tests; i) BUYER’s standby costs.

在合同规定的质保期内，如果所交付的货物质量、规格与合同规定不符或不符合技术协议的规格，或者因任何原因，包括潜在的瑕疵或者使用不适当的材料被证明存在缺陷，买方应安排与卖方进行联合检验。如果卖方被认定负有责任，则所有相关费用应由卖方承担，包括但不限于:a)派遣卖方检验员进行联合检查的费用；b)上述货物的拆卸和搬运费用（如适用）c)将有缺陷的合同货物退回到卖方进行修理或更换；d)修理或更换的费用；e)所有相关测试和重新认证的费用；f)交付修理后或更换货物的费用；g)搬运和安装的费用；h)额外的现场验收测试；i）买方因此停窝工造成的费用。
