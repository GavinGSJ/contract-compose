## Each package of CONTRACT GOODS shall be attached with detailed packing list in two (2) copies and each kind of goods, especially spare parts, accessories and special tools shall be attached with an individual label indicating description and identification number.

合同货物的每份包装应附上两份详细的装箱单复印件，备品备件、附件和专用工具应单独附上与货物描述和标识号一致的标签。
