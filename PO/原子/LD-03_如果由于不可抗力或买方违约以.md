## Failure to provide TECHNICAL SERVICE according to requirement 未按要求提供技术服务

If, for any reason other than Force Majeure or BUYER’s default, SELLER fails to carry out TECHNICAL SERVICE according to the timeline and requirements specified in TECHNICAL AGREEMENT, SELLER shall pay BUYER liquidated damages at the rate of {{迟交违约金_前四周}}% of the TOTAL CONTRACT PRICE for every seven (7) days of delay or non-compliance for the first four (4) weeks of such delay or non-compliance, and thereafter at a rate of {{迟交违约金_此后}}% of the TOTAL CONTRACT PRICE for every seven (7) days of such delay or non-compliance. The liquidated damages for failure to provide TECHNICAL SERVICE shall not exceed {{服务违约金_上限}}% of the TOTAL CONTRACT PRICE.

如果由于不可抗力或买方违约以外的任何原因，卖方未能按照技术协议中规定的时间和要求提供技术服务，卖方应在此类延误或不合规的前四（4）周内，每延误七（7）天或不合规，向买方支付总价的百分之{{中文数字:迟交违约金_前四周}}（{{迟交违约金_前四周}}%）的违约赔偿金，此后每延误七天或不合规，应向买方支付总额的百分之{{中文数字:迟交违约金_此后}}（{{迟交违约金_此后}}%）的违约金。未按要求提供技术服务的违约赔偿金不得超过总价的百分之{{中文数字:服务违约金_上限}}（{{服务违约金_上限}}%）。
