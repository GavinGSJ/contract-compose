## In case any wooden package is used, SELLER shall also mark the relevant information include the IPPC Logo, Country Code, Unique number assigned by NPPO to the producer or the WPM, and the method of the treatment (“HT” for heat treatment or “MB” for methyl bromide), etc. with fadeless paint on each package.

如果有木制包装，卖方应在每个包装上用不褪色的油漆颜料标注相关信息，包括IPPC 标志，国家代码，NPPO 签发给生产者或WPM的编码，处理方式（热处理“HT”或溴甲烷熏蒸处理“MB”）等。
