## SELLER shall obtain and maintain any licenses, permits and approvals for the performance of the SUPPLY in accordance with APPLICABLE LAW. Where the SELLER is responsible for the renting, hiring, purchasing, importing, exporting, delivering or otherwise using the SELLER Items in connection with the CONTRACT (including obtaining any necessary customs clearance or other authorization required from any authority for moving such items into and out of any jurisdiction), the SELLER shall comply with all APPLICABLE LAW (including in respect of security).

卖方应根据适用法律获得并维护执行供货的任何许可、许可和批准。如果卖方负责租赁、雇佣、购买、进口、出口、交付或以其他方式使用与合同相关的卖方物品（包括从任何当局获得任何必要的清关或其他授权，以将此类物品移入或移出任何管辖区），卖方应遵守所有适用法律（包括有关安全的法律）。
