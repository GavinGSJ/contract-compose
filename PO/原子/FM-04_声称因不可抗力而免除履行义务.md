## A PARTY claiming that its performance is excused by FORCE MAJEURE shall, promptly within 3 days after the occurrence of FORCE MAJEURE, notify the other PARTY (and, as soon as reasonably possible but no later than within 7 days thereafter, provide such other PARTY with satisfactory supporting evidence) of the nature, date of inception and expected duration of the FORCE MAJEURE and the extent to which the PARTY expects that the event will delay, prevent or hinder the PARTY from performing its obligations under this CONTRACT. The notifying PARTY shall thereafter use its reasonable efforts to eliminate the FORCE MAJEURE and mitigate its effects.

声称因不可抗力而免除履行义务的一方应在不可抗力发生后3天内，立即通知另一方（并在合理可能的情况下尽快但不迟于其后7天内，向该另一方提供令人满意的支持证据），不可抗力的开始日期和预计持续时间，以及该方预计该事件将延迟、阻止或阻碍该方履行本合同项下义务的程度。此后，通知方应尽其合理努力消除不可抗力并减轻其影响。
