## SELLER shall immediately notify BUYER in the event it becomes aware of any circumstances that could result in a claim or potential claim under any indemnity offered by BUYER in favor of any member of SELLER GROUP under this CONTRACT. In such circumstances BUYER may elect to conduct the defense or participate in the defense of any such claim and SELLER GROUP shall comply with any of the reasonable directions of BUYER in this regard. No member of SELLER GROUP shall act, or fail to act, in any way so as to compromise or prejudice the defense of the claim. SELLER shall not authorize the settlement of any such claims without the prior written consent of BUYER. Where SELLER fails to carry out any action required by BUYER in relation to the foregoing, BUYER may take any such action on its own behalf and shall be entitled to full reimbursement for any resulting costs from SELLER. Should SELLER be prevented from performing any part of the SUPPLY hereunder by reason of legal proceedings based upon such claim, BUYER shall be relieved of its obligation to make payment for such part of the SUPPLY not performed as a result thereof.

如果卖方意识到任何可能导致买方根据本合同向卖方集团任何成员提供的任何赔偿提出索赔或潜在索赔的情况，卖方应立即通知买方。在这种情况下，买方可选择对任何此类索赔进行辩护或参与辩护，卖方集团应遵守买方在这方面的任何合理指示。卖方集团的任何成员不得以任何方式采取或不采取行动，以损害或损害索赔的辩护。未经买方事先书面同意，卖方无权擅自解决任何此类索赔。如果卖方未能执行买方要求的与上述内容相关的任何行动，买方可自行采取任何此类行动，并有权从卖方处获得任何由此产生的费用的全额补偿。如果卖方因此类索赔的法律程序而无法执行本合同项下的任何供货，则相应解除买方就该部分供货的支付义务。
