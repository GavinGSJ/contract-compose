## SELLER shall, at SELLER’s cost, be liable for and shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless BUYER GROUP against any and all INDEMNIFIED COSTS in respect of or arising out of any matter set out in relation to liabilities which SELLER has assumed under the CONTRACT. BUYER shall, at BUYER’S cost, be liable for and shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless SELLER GROUP against any and all INDEMNIFIED COSTS in respect of or arising out of any matter set out in relation to liabilities which BUYER has assumed under the CONTRACT. Without prejudice to the provisions of this Article, SELLER shall be liable for and defend, indemnify and hold harmless BUYER GROUP against any INDEMNIFIED COSTS of whatsoever nature arising from liens or attachments by any person in connection with or arising out of the performance of this CONTRACT and BUYER may recover such sum as a debt.

卖方应自费承担买方集团因卖方在本合同项下承担的责任相关的任何事项而产生的任何和所有赔偿费用，并对买方集团进行辩护、赔偿并使其免受损害。买方应自费承担卖方集团因买方根据本合同承担的责任而产生的任何和所有赔偿费用，并对卖方集团进行辩护、赔偿并使其免受损害。在不影响本条规定的情况下，卖方应负责保护、赔偿买方集团，使其免受因任何人因履行本合同而产生的留置权而产生的任何性质的赔偿费用，买方可将该款项作为债务收回。
