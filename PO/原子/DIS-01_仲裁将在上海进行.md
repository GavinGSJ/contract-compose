## The arbitration shall take place in Shanghai. The arbitration tribunal shall consist of three (3) arbitrators. Each PARTY shall nominate an arbitrator and the arbitrators so nominated shall nominate the third arbitrator who shall act as the chairman of the tribunal. The arbitration language including documents shall be in English.

仲裁将在上海进行。仲裁委员会由三人组成，双方各指定一名仲裁员，并由这两名仲裁员指定第三名仲裁员，作为仲裁委员会主席。仲裁语言，包括资料应为英文。
