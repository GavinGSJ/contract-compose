## In the event of any dispute whatsoever, SELLER shall not suspend or stop any supply or commissioning work, and shall continue to perform its obligation under this CONTRACT until completion or until the matter is finally determined under the dispute resolution provided in this CONTRACT. Breach of this Article shall be deemed a material breach.

在发生任何争议的情况下，卖方不得暂停或停止任何供货或试运行工作，并应继续履行其在本合同项下的义务，直至完工或根据本合同规定的争议解决方式对该事项做出最终裁决。违反本条款应被视为重大违约。
