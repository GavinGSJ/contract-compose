## SELLER shall be responsible for the care, custody, control, protection and/or preservation of the CONTRACT GOODS up to the point when the CONTRACT GOODS has been delivered to BUYER.

卖方应负责照管、保管、控制、保护和/或保存合同货物，直至合同货物交付给买方。
