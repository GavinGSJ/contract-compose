## SELLER is fully responsible for adequate preparation for shipping to assure the CONTRACT GOODS safe arrival at the SITE. All costs, including return freight for items returned to the SELLER (such as saddles, bolsters, or shipping braces) should be included within the TOTAL CONTRACT PRICE, and it is the responsibility of the SELLER for return arrangements.

卖方完全负责装运的充分准备工作，以确保合同货物安全到达场地。所有费用，包括部件退还给卖方的返程运费（如托运、垫木、或装运拉条），都应包含在合同总价中，并且须由卖方负责退还安排。
