## Except to the extent otherwise stated in this Article, all exclusions and indemnities given under this Article shall apply irrespective of cause, and notwithstanding the negligence or breach of duty (whether statutory or otherwise) of the indemnified PARTY or any other entity or party and shall apply irrespective of any claims in tort, under this CONTRACT or APPLICABLE LAW. However, other than as provided for in Article {{ref:IND-07}} the exclusion of liability and indemnities shall not apply and may not be relied on by either Party to the extent that any claim or liability was caused by willful misconduct or fraud.

除非本第{{ref:IND-00}}条另有规定，在本合同条款或适用法律规定下，第{{ref:IND-00}}条列明的所有除外责任和赔偿均应适用，无论受偿方或任何其他实体或当事方的疏忽或失职（法定或其他），应适用于任何侵权索赔。但是，除第{{ref:IND-07}}条规定的情况外，如果任何索赔或责任是由故意不当行为或欺诈造成的，则责任和赔偿的排除不适用，任何一方都不得再以此条款作为依照。
