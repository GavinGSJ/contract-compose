## The CONTRACT GOODS shall be carefully and strongly packed by SELLER with shock resistant packing materials according to their shapes and different features so as to withstand moisture, rain, shock and numerous handlings during loading, unloading and long-distance, ocean, land and air transportation as the case may take place.

合同货物应由卖方根据货物的不同形状和特点对货物进行小心安全的牢固的包装，保证货物防潮、防雨、防锈、防腐蚀和防震等，从而适合多次搬运、装卸以及长途海运及内陆运输和空运等可能发生的运输情况。
