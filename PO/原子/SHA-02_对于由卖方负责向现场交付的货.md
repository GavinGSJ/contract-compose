## For GOODS delivery to SITE that is under SELLER’s responsibility, SELLER shall, within forty-eight (48) hours after the completion of the loading of the CONTRACT GOODS, advise the BUYER and BUYER’s shipping agent at the port of destination or email of the CONTRACT No., name of GOODS (strictly follow the CONTRACT), quantity, total volume, gross weight, net weight, invoice number and value, date and number of Bill of lading, name of the vessel and estimated date of arrival. In case the BUYER fails to arrange insurance in time due to the SELLER not providing the necessary and correct information in time, all the losses, including the costs and expenses related thereto (including the value of the GOODS) shall be borne by the SELLER.

对于由卖方负责向现场交付的货物，卖方应在合同货物完成装货后48小时内，以邮件方式通知买方及买方的目的港货代，包括合同号、货物品名（严格按照合同）、数量、总值、毛重、净重、发票号及金额、提单日期及号码、船名及预计到港时间等信息。如因卖方未及时通知必要和正确的信息而导致买方未能及时投保，卖方应承担因此所产生的一切损失，包括相关成本和费用（包括货物价值）。
