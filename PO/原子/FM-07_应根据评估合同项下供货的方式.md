## In case of termination, BUYER 在终止的情况下，买方

- shall pay SELLER the amount due under the CONTRACT for the part of CONTRACT GOODS completed by SELLER and accepted by Buyer prior to the date of termination as agreed between the PARTIES based on means of evaluating the SUPPLY under the CONTRACT, and

  应根据评估合同项下供货的方式，向卖方支付本合同项下卖方在合同终止日期前完成且买方接受的合同货物的应付款项，且

- after deduction of the completed payments, on a pro rata basis, for all uncompleted SUPPLY shall reimburse SELLER, upon presentation of satisfactory supporting documents, for all costs reasonably， directly and irrevocably incurred and paid in good faith in respect of the uncompleted parts of the SUPPLY, it being understood that SELLER shall have the obligation to minimize reimbursable costs.

  扣除所有未完成供货的未付款后，应在提交令人满意的证明文件后，按比例偿还卖方因供货未完成部分而合理、直接且不可撤销地产生和善意支付的所有费用，双方理解，卖方有义务将已发生费用降至最低。

Should FORCE MAJEURE affect only a part of the SUPPLY, BUYER shall be entitled to withdraw such part from the CONTRACT, and the provisions stipulated here above shall apply mutatis mutandis to such part, and the CONTRACT shall remain in force for those parts of the SUPPLY unaffected by such FORCE MAJEURE. In such case, BUYER shall determine the reduction in the TOTAL CONTRACT PRICE to reflect the omission based on the value of the CONTRACT GOODS in the withdrawn and remaining parts calculated by reference to the TOTAL CONTRACT PRICE.

如果不可抗力仅影响供货的一部分，买方有权从合同中撤回该部分，上述规定应比照适用于该部分，合同对不受不可抗力影响的供货部分仍然有效。在这种情况下，买方应参照合同总价计算的撤回和剩余部件中合同货物的价值，确定合同总价的减少，以反映该等撤回。
