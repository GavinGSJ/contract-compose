## DELIVERY DATE OF CONTRACT GOODS 交货时间:

On or before {{交货日期_en}}, {{贸易术语_en}}

不迟于{{交货日期}}，{{贸易术语}}

For delivery in several batches, the DELIVERY DATE stipulated above is the contractual DELIVERY DATE of the last batch of shipment. The contractual delivery dates for all the batches could be found in ATTACHMENT B.

对于分批交货的情况，上述交货日期为最后一批货物的交货日期。所有批次的合同交货日期见附件B。

Partial shipment or batch deliveries, if it has not been agreed upon in the CONTRACT, shall be allowed only at BUYER’s sole discretion upon SELLER’s request.

如果合同中没有约定，只有在卖方提出要求的情况下，买方可自行决定是否允许分批装运或分批交付。

Note: SELLER shall not ship the CONTRACT GOODS without BUYER’s consent. Violation of this requirement shall constitute a rejection of the CONTRACT GOODS, BUYER may return or take other actions deemed required by BUYER that all costs associated costs shall be borne by the SELLER.

说明：如果没有经过买方的同意，卖方一定不能装运此合同中的货物。若违反这一规定，买方可以拒收货物、退货或采取买方认为需要的其他行动，并由卖方承担相关费用。
