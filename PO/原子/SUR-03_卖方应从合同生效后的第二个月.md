## SELLER shall submit monthly progress report on every 25th of the month starting from the second month after the CONTRACT becomes effective until the completion of delivery of the CONTRACT GOODS based on the template given in this CONTRACT to the designated BUYER’s representative(s) by email. If late delivery is detected, SELLER shall submit weekly progress reports (" Weekly Report") and one-week look ahead plan to BUYER, if required by BUYER. In addition, SELLER shall immediately provide corrective or mitigation plan to avoid or mitigate such delay for BUYER’s review. Upon acceptance, SELLER must immediately execute such corrective or mitigation plan. Failing so, BUYER may perform the SUPPLY or a part thereof or have it performed by others, with any costs consequent or incidental thereto shall be borne by the SELLER.

卖方应从合同生效后的第二个月开始，每月25日通过邮件向买方指定的代表提交月度进度报告，直至合同物资交付完毕。如果发现延迟交货，卖方应按买方要求向买方提交每周进度报告（“周报”）和次周计划。此外，卖方应立即提供纠正或减轻影响的措施供买方审查，以避免或减轻此类延迟。一经接受，卖方必须立即执行。否则，买方可自行或交由他人实施供货或部分供货，由此产生或附带的任何费用应由卖方承担。

As a minimum this progress report shall include the following:

此进度报告最少应包括下列：

+ Engineering schedule, including date of submission and desired return of SELLER’s drawings, date of Classification Society drawings sent for review and returned.

  设计进度表，包括卖方图纸提交日期及希望返回的日期，船级社图纸送审及退审的日期。

+ Material procurement schedule containing SELLER’s suborder dates, sub-supplier’s name and expected material delivery dates.

  材料采购进度表，包括卖方分订单签订日期，分供应商名称及预期材料交付日期

+ Fabrication schedule containing:

  制造进度表，包含：

  + Start and complete dates of major points in the production schedule, including but not limited to plate cutting, welding, assembly, and other manufacturing processes.

    生产进度主要节点开始与完成日期，包括但不限于下料，焊接，组装等制造工艺；

  + Starting and completion dates of each test and inspection point during fabrication, including but not limited to: pressure test, NDT inspections, and inspection points H or W included in the ITP.

    制造期间各试验及检验点的开始与完成日期，包括但不限于：压力试验，NDT检验，以及ITP所包含的H或W的检验点；

  + Shipping date (s); shipment location(s); transportation way

    发货日期、发运地、运输方式等；

  + Pictures of the product (s)

    产品照片；
