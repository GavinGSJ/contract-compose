## Quality Requirements: Conform to the design documents (drawings, specifications or other technical information) confirmed by both parties and the signed ATTACHMENT C: TECHNICAL AGREEMENT.

质量要求：符合双方确认的设计文件（图纸、规格书或其它技术资料）和已签署的附件C:《技术协议》。
