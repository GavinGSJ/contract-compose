## CONTRACT LANGUAGE 合同语言

The CONTRACT is made in both English and Chinese languages. The English version of this CONTRACT shall prevail in case of conflict with Chinese version. All communications between the parties shall be written in English during execution of CONTRACT, inclusive for all correspondences and document submitted for TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION.

本合同以中、英文书就，如中英文版本有冲突，以英文版本为准。在合同执行期间，双方之间的一切联系应以英语书写，包括所有通信和提交的技术文件。

This CONTRACT will be made in 2 original copies. BUYER shall keep 1 copies while SELLER shall keep the other 1 copies.

本合同一式2份，买方执1份，卖方执1份。
