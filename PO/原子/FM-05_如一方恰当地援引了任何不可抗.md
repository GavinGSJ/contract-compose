## A PARTY that has properly invoked an event of FORCE MAJEURE shall promptly within 3 days notify the other PARTY of termination of any such event of FORCE MAJEURE, whereupon performance of this CONTRACT shall recommence. Notwithstanding any other provision of this CONTRACT, during the period of any such non-performance, each PARTY shall be liable for and bear all of its own costs, expenses, losses and damages suffered and incurred as a result of a circumstance or event of FORCE MAJEURE and the period of any such nonperformance, together with such period as may be necessary for the restoration of any damage, shall be added to the time allowed in this CONTRACT for the performance excused by the event of FORCE MAJEURE.

Each PARTY shall be responsible for their own direct and indirect financial consequences resulted from any FORCE MAJEURE. BUYER shall not be paying SELLER additional costs or compensation for SELLER's suspension of works due to FORCE MAJEURE.

如一方恰当地援引了任何不可抗力，应在此不可抗力事件终止后的3天内立即通知另一方，然后重新开始履行本合同。尽管本合同有任何其他规定，在任何此类不可抗力原因所导致的无法履约期间，双方应各自负责并承担因不可抗力情况或事件而遭受的所有成本、费用、损失和损害，并且后续恢复任何相关损害所需的时间成本，应添加到本合同允许的因不可抗力事件而免除的履约时间中。

各方应分别承担此类不可抗力造成的所有直接和间接财务后果，买方不得因不可抗力导致工程暂停而向卖方支付额外款项或赔偿。
