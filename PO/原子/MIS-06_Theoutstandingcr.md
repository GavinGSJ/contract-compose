## The outstanding credit and debt between the parties under CONTRACT shall not be affected upon the termination or expiration of the CONTRACT, unless otherwise provided by the law or agreed by the PARTIES. 双方在合同项下的未付款项不受合同终止或到期的影响(法律或合同另有约定的除外)。
