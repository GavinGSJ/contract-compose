## Operations and all activities undertaken at the facilities of the BUYER, as well as the facilities of the SELLER if not otherwise governed by SELLER’s HSSE policy, shall be governed by this Article.在买方设施以及卖方设施现场进行的操作和所有活动，如果不受卖方HSSE政策的约束，则应遵守本条款。
