# ASSIGNMENT AND NOVATION 转让与替换

Title of the GOODS shall be free of any lien, encumbrance or pledge.

货物的所有权不存在任何留置权、产权负担或质押。

SELLER may not assign or novate the CONTRACT, in full or in part, without the prior consent of BUYER.

未经买方事先同意，卖方不得全部或部分转让或替换合同。

BUYER may assign or novate the CONTRACT in full or in part, to any third party.

买方可将合同全部或部分转让给任何第三方。

The PARTIES agree to execute such documents as may be necessary to effect the permitted assignment or novation. Any assignment or novation in violation of the above shall be void and without effect for the other party.

双方同意签署必要的文件，以实现允许的转让或主体替换。任何违反上述规定的转让或更替对另一方均无效。

The PARTIES expressly agree and acknowledge (for avoidance of any doubt) that this CONTRACT is assignable, at BUYER’s option, to OWNER in the event of termination, and that any warranties against defects in this CONTRACT shall be assignable to OWNER upon BUYER’s written notice.

双方明确同意并承认（为避免任何疑问），在合同终止时，本合同可由买方选择转让给业主，且本合同中的任何缺陷担保应根据买方的书面通知转让给业主。
