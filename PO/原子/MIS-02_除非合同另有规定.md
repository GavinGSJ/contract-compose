## SELLER PERSONNEL 卖方人员

SELLER must, unless otherwise specified in the CONTRACT, ensure that their PERSONNEL, competent, professional and are dedicated for the performance of the SUPPLY on a full time basis.

除非合同另有规定，否则卖方必须确保其人员称职、专业并全职履行供货。

BUYER may object to and require SELLER to remove any person, directly or indirectly employed for the performance of the SUPPLY, by SELLER or any SUBCONTRACTOR, whose behavior has been reprehensible or uncooperative, or who is lacking competence, is negligent, is guilty of misconduct, is in breach of any of BUYER’s policies (including but not limited to the Health, Safety, Environment and Security requirements) or whose presence is otherwise considered not to be in the best interests of the SUPPLY. If BUYER so requires, SELLER must immediately have such person replaced by a competent substitute acceptable to BUYER at no extra cost to BUYER.

买方可以反对并要求卖方立即调离卖方或任何分包商直接或间接为此供货雇佣的行为应受谴责的或不配合的、或能力不能胜任、粗心、有不当行为的、或违反买方政策（包括但不限于健康、安全、环境和保密要求）、或买方认为其存在将不利于供货的人员。如果买方要求将这些人替换成买方认可的能胜任的人员，则卖方必须立即执行，且不得要求买方支付任何额外费用。
