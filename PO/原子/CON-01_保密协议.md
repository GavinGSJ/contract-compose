## All communications and any manner of disclosure between the PARTIES or among members of their respective Groups shall be governed by the signed NDA (ATTACHMENT I).

买卖双方之间或各自专家组的成员之间的所有通信和任何形式的信息均应遵守已签署的保密附件。（附件I）
