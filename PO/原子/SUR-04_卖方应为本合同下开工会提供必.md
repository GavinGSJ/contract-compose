## SELLER shall provide the following necessary information (1,2,3,4,5), but not limited to, before Kick-off Meeting. Details please refer to the CONTRACT attachments (Kick-off Meeting PPT template). And SELLER shall also participate in the Kick-Off meeting requested by the BUYER.

卖方应为本合同下开工会提供必要的信息如下。具体要求请参见合同附件（开工会PPT模板）。同时，卖方还应按照买方要求参加开工会。

+ Supplier’s Project Organization Chart - Refer to attached KOM PPT

  供应商项目组织架构图 - 参考附件开工会PPT模板

+ Overall Execution Schedule

  整体执行计划

+ Vendor Documentation List with submission date

  供应商文件清单及提交日期

+ ITP & FAT Procedures (if applicable)

  ITP和FAT程序文件（如适用）

+ Others requested by BUYER (if applicable)

  买方的其他要求（如适用）
