## The SELLER shall be liable for any extra cost for the BUYER arranging shipment if the shipping advice or correct shipping information or packing information are not provided to the BUYER according to the contract.

如因卖方未按照规定及时提供发运通知或未提供正确的发运、包装信息导致买方为安排运输而产生额外费用应由卖方承担。
