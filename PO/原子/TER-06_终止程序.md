## Termination procedure. Upon termination of this CONTRACT, the SELLER shall follow all reasonable instructions issued by BUYER to give effect to such termination including as necessary to arrange hand over to BUYER of any CONTRACT GOODS or other aspects of the SUPPLY (including technical documents) directed by BUYER.

终止程序。本合同终止后，卖方应遵守买方发出的所有合理指示，以使得终止生效，包括必要时安排向买方移交任何买方指示的合同货物或供货的其他方面（包括技术文件）。
