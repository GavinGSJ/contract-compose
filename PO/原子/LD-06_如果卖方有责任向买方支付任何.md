## In case the SELLER is liable for paying to the BUYER any liquidated damages, the payment shall be made by T/T within ten (10) days after SELLER has received the claim from BUYER. Otherwise, BUYER will have the right to deduct the amount of liquidated damages from payment to be made to SELLER, or to recover such an amount as much as possible from the performance bond, warranty bond or retention fund, whichever is possible. If there is any unrecoverable amount from the above mentioned means, BUYER shall have the right to seek for such compensation from SELLER in accordance with Article {{ref:DIS-00}} of this CONTRACT.

如果卖方有责任向买方支付任何违约赔偿金，则卖方应在收到买方的索赔通知后10天内通过电汇支付，否则买方有权从将支付给卖方的任何款项中扣除该金额，或尽可能从履约保函、质保函或质保金中收回违约赔偿金。如果通过上述方式仍有任何无法收回的款项，买方有权按照本合同第{{ref:DIS-00}}条的约定向卖方要求赔偿。
