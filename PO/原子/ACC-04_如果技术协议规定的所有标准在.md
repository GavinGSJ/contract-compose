## If all the criteria stipulated in TECHNICAL AGREEMENT have been fulfilled during the new performance test / SAT, an Acceptance Certificate of the CONTRACT GOODS shall be issued by BUYER and signed by the authorized representatives of the PARTIES within three (3) working days in four original copies, two copies will be kept by each PARTY for safekeeping. This signing of the Acceptance Certificate” shall evidence the acceptance to the CONTRACT GOODS by BUYER.

如果技术协议规定的所有标准在新的性能试验/现场验收试验期间全部达标，则买方应在三 (3) 个工作日内签发合同货物验收证书，并由双方授权代表签署，正本一式四份，双方各执两份。签署 "验收证书 "应证明买方已接受合同货物。

However, if the performance test / SAT failed again due to reasons attributed to SELLER, SELLER shall immediately refund the amount already paid from BUYER to SELLER for the said CONTRACT GOODS, and the said CONTRACT GOODS will become the property of SELLER after BUYER fully received the payment. In that case, if any further losses suffered by the BUYER from the sourcing activities, including the cost difference between the TOTAL CONTRACT PRICE herein and the cost of procuring alternative/replacement goods from any third party, shall be recoverable from the SELLER.

如果由于卖方原因导致性能试验/现场验收试验再次失败，卖方应立即归还买方就上述合同项下货物已经支付给卖方的款项，款项足额归还买方后货物的所有权转移为卖方。在此情况下，如果买方因此而遭受任何进一步损失，包括本合同总价与从任何第三方采购替代/替换货物的成本直接的差额，应向卖方追偿。

All consumables or special tools required during the performance test / SAT shall be furnished by SELLER at SELLER’s costs. In the event that the spare parts purchased by the BUYER is required to be used, upon BUYER’s consent, SELLER may use the spare parts already purchased by the BUYER and shall immediately replenish it within a reasonable time at SELLER’s own cost.

性能试验/现场验收试验期间所需的所有消耗品或特殊工具应由卖方提供，费用由卖方承担。如果需要使用买方购买的备件，经买方同意，卖方可以使用买方已经购买的备件，并应立即在合理的时间内补充，费用由卖方承担。
