## SELLER shall be liable for any damage and loss of the CONTRACT GOODS and the expenses incurred on account of improper packing and for any rust attributable to inadequate or improper protective measures taken by SELLER in regard to the packing.

卖方应承担由于包装不当而造成的货物损坏、丢失的费用以及由于包装不当或包装保护不到位而造成的货物生锈、损坏等的责任。
