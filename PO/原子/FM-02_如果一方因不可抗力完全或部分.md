## To the extent that, and for the period of time during which, a PARTY is fully or partially delayed, prevented or hindered by FORCE MAJEURE from performing any obligation under this CONTRACT (other than an obligation to make payment) who has given such notice to the other PARTY in accordance with Article {{ref:FM-04}}, despite the exercise of reasonable diligence by the affected PARTY, the failure to perform shall be excused by the occurrence and to the extent of such event of FORCE MAJEURE.

如果一方因不可抗力完全或部分延迟、阻止或阻碍履行本合同项下的任何义务（付款义务除外），并根据第{{ref:FM-04}}条约定及时通知了另一方，且在该段时间内，尽管受影响方已尽了合理的努力，但该方未能履行义务的情况，应视为不可抗力事件发生，免除其在不可抗力影响下的相关责任。
