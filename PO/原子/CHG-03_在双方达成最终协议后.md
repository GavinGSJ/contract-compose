## When final agreement is reached by PARTIES, an amendment to the CONTRACT will be issued to formalize the changes to the CONTRACT. Such an amendment shall form an integral part of CONTRACT, and shall have the same legal force as the text of CONTRACT. On-going discussion on the potential changes, initiated either by the BUYER or the SELLER, must ensure that it shall not affect the continuous execution of the existing CONTRACT.

在双方达成最终协议后，将发布合同修正案，正式确定对合同的修改。该修正案应视为合同完整的组成部分，与合同正文具有同样的法律效力。无论是买方还是卖方提出的潜在变更，在商议过程中，必须确保原合同的持续执行不受影响。
