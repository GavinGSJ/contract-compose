## Notwithstanding anything contained in this CONTRACT to the contrary, SELLER’s total liability in respect of any and all claims for damages or losses shall not exceed 100% of the TOTAL CONTRACT PRICE. For the avoidance of doubt, such liability limitation shall not apply to: (i) liabilities or Claims arising from infringement or violations of any above-said INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS of any third party; (ii) costs and expenses related to repairs, correction or replacement of defects of GOODS; (iii) any obligations of the SELLER to indemnify third party; (iv) any obligations or liabilities assumed by the SELLER under Articles {{ref:CON-00}} (Confidentiality Responsibility), {{ref:IND-00}} (Indemnities and Insurance), {{ref:TAX-00}} (Taxes), ATTACHMENT G (Compliance: Administrative and anti-corruption liability, Corporate Social Responsibility and Human Rights) or ATTACHMENT K (Undertaking of Honest Conduct), (v) any or all proceeds recovered by the SELLER from the insurances in accordance with Article {{ref:IND-12}}; and (vi) in the case of fraud, misrepresentation, gross negligence, willful misconduct, abandonment or illegal or unlawful acts by the SELLER;

即便本合同或有任何冲突规定，卖方在履行合同或未履行合同中给买方造成任何损失或损害的总的赔偿责任，不得超过合同总价的100%。 为避免歧义，该等责任限制不应适用以下情形：（1）因侵犯或违反第三方知识产权而产生的责任、索赔；（2）修理、纠正或替代瑕疵货物相关的成本和费用；（3）卖方赔偿第三方的费用；（4）卖方根据第{{ref:CON-00}}条（保密责任）、第{{ref:IND-00}}条（赔偿和保险）、第{{ref:TAX-00}}条（税收）、附件G（合规行政和反腐败责任、企业社会责任和人权）或附件K（廉洁从业承诺书）应承担的任何义务或责任，（5）卖方根据第{{ref:IND-12}}条从保险中收回的任何或所有收益，及（6）由于卖方欺诈、误导性陈述、重大疏忽、恶意行为、违法或不法行为导致的损失。
