## (a) Should the SUPPLY supplied by the SELLER be found defective (except normal wear and tear) during the warranty period, as described in this Article {{ref:QUA-00}}, the BUYER shall inform the SELLER immediately by fax or email. The BUYER’s notice shall be considered effective if given within the warranty period plus thirty (30) days after the expiration of the warranty period. The SELLER shall within forty-eight（48）hours after receiving the BUYER’s notice commence to rectify such notified defect and shall dispatch all necessary PERSONNEL and resources to the PROJECT or other relevant site(s) to perform such rectification SUPPLY.

卖方提供的供货在合同项下第{{ref:QUA-00}}条内所阐明的质保期内出现质量问题（不包括正常损耗），买方应以传真或email方式及时通知卖方。买方在质保期满后30天内通知仍被视为有效。卖方应在收到通知后48小时内开始处理通知中所提及的缺陷，并向项目或其他相关现场派遣所有必要的人员和资源，以执行改正修缮工作。

(b) If after having been notified of any defect in the SUPPLY, the SELLER fails to promptly remedy the defects within a reasonable time determined by BUYER, the BUYER shall have the right, after first giving the SELLER not less than three (3) working days written notice of its intention, to rectify, repair or replace the defective SUPPLY itself or to have them replaced or repaired by others, and all costs of such rectification, repair or replacement shall be borne by the SELLER. The exercise by the BUYER of this right shall in no way relieve the SELLER from its obligations under the Contract.

在接到有关供货缺陷的通知后，如卖方在合理时间内未能有效解决该缺陷的，买方有权对缺陷部分自行或者委托第三方进行维修或者更换，且产生的维修更换费用等由卖方承担，上述行为买方应至少提前三天书面通知卖方。卖方在本合同下的相关责任和义务不会因此而免除。

(c) Without limiting BUYER’s rights and remedies as set forth in Article {{ref:QUA-06}}(b), as an alternative to any rectification work being carried out by the SELLER, particularly when operational constraints prevent appropriate access to the CONTRACT GOODS, the BUYER may, provided that it does so in a reasonable manner and in consultation with the SELLER, carry out, or arrange for others to carry out, the necessary rectification work at the SELLER’s expense. Following such repairs or replacements, the SELLER shall promptly reimburse the BUYER for all the costs of such rectification work. The exercise by the BUYER of this option shall, except as may be specifically agreed otherwise, in no way relieve the SELLER from its obligations under the Contract.

在不限制第{{ref:QUA-06}}（b）条中规定的买方权利和补救措施的情况下，作为由卖方进行修理或更换工作的一种替代，尤其是因操作原因不能触碰到合同货物时，在与卖方进行合理咨询与协商后买方可以在卖方承担费用的情况下自行或安排他人进行必要工作。修理或更换工作进行后，卖方应立即偿还买方所有这些工作的成本。除非另有特别约定，买方行使上述权利不免除卖方在合同项下的义务。
