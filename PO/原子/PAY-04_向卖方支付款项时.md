## For payments to SELLER, BUYER shall be entitled to reject the choice of bank account designated by SELLER with reason(s). Such bank account shall be in SELLER’s name and shall be maintained either in the country of performance of the SUPPLY or in SELLER’s domicile. All banking related charges incurred by the BUYER's bank shall be borne by the BUYER and charges incurred by the SELLER's bank shall be borne by the SELLER.

向卖方支付款项时，买方有权基于合理理由拒绝选择卖方指定的银行账户。该账户名应与本合同卖方名称保持一致，并在供货实施地国家或卖方所在地注册。买方银行产生的所有银行相关费用由买方承担，卖方银行产生的费用由卖方承担。
