## Suspended due to the suspension of the PROJECT, the SELLER shall, upon receipt of the written notice of the suspension, stop the execution of this CONTRACT and shall preserve and protect the materials including and CONTRACT GOODS under this CONTRACT. The SELLER shall not charge any additional fee if the CONTRACT is suspended for a period not exceeding ({{暂停免费天数}}) days. In the case of a suspension of more than ({{暂停免费天数}}) days, other than for reasons attributable to SELLER GROUP, the storage or maintenance costs arising thereafter shall be determined by mutual agreement. Any costs arising from any suspension for reasons attributable to SELLER GROUP shall be borne by the SELLER.

SELLER and BUYER shall agree on the terms of the SUPPLY resumption, upon receiving the written notification from BUYER, within (15) days. SELLER’s sole remedy for the negotiation and execution of resumption, if agreed by BUYER, shall be an extension of time up to (15) days.

如果本合同的执行因项目暂停而暂缓履行，卖方应在收到暂停的书面通知后停止执行本合同，并应保存和保护本合同项下的材料，包括合同货物。如果合同暂停不超过（{{暂停免费天数}}）天，卖方不得收取任何额外费用。如果暂停超过（{{暂停免费天数}}）天，除卖方集团的原因外，由此产生的储存或维护费用应由双方协商确定。因卖方集团原因导致的任何暂停产生的任何费用应由卖方承担。

在收到买方的书面通知后（15）天内，卖方和买方应就供货恢复的条款达成一致。如果买方同意，卖方就此恢复供货的协商和执行可获得的唯一救济应为交付延期，且延期时间不应超过15天。
