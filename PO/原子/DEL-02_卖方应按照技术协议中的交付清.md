## Delivery of TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION 文件资料交付

SELLER shall deliver TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION in accordance with list of deliverables in the TECHNICAL AGREEMENT and the given timeframe following the relevant procedure(s) in this CONTRACT.

卖方应按照技术协议中的交付清单和本合同中的相关程序规定的时限交付技术文件。

The final TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION, in the number of hardcopy and/or softcopy specified in the CONTRACT, shall be delivered by SELLER based on DDP terms to the shipment address provided by the BUYER with all cost related to the delivery shall be borne by the SELLER, inclusive of the international courier service charges. The content and delivery schedule of this final TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION, shall comply with the TECHNICAL AGREEMENT.

卖方应根据DDP条款，将最终技术资料按合同规定的硬拷贝和/或软拷贝的数量交付到买方提供的装运地址。与交付有关的所有费用均应由卖方承担，包括国际快递服务费。最终技术资料内容和交付时间进度应遵循技术协议的要求。

SELLER shall notify BUYER by email within seventy-two (72) hours after dispatching the final TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION. In case of any loss, shortage or damage, SELLER shall make the replacement and resend them to BUYER again free of charge within three (3) days after receiving BUYER’s notification.

卖方应在技术文件发送后72小时内通过邮件通知买方。一旦在运输中出现短缺、丢失或受损，卖方应在收到买方通知的3天内免费更换并重新发送给买方。
