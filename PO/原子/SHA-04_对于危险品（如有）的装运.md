## For shipment of hazardous goods if any, SELLER shall, thirty (30) days before the date of delivery stipulated in the Contract, provide the BUYER with full information including all appropriate shipping certification as required for ocean, land and air transportation and port operation upon arrival at the destination and instructions to the BUYER and the other party for safety handling, transit and storage, exposure and disposal in a form sufficiently to identify all actions, such as:

对于危险品（如有）的装运，卖方应在合同约定的交货期前30天向买方提供包括海洋运输、内陆运输和空运以及目的港码头操作需要的运输证明以及给买方及其他第三方用于安全操作、转运、储存、展示以及处置用的操作说明，例如：

Materials Safety Data Sheet (MSDS), and 材料安全数据表（MSDS）及

Dangerous goods label as required by shipment and storage, and so on.运输和储存用的危险货物标签等。
