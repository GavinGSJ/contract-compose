## For all oversized cargos, if any piece of GOODS exceeds ten (10) metric tons in weight, nine (9) meters in length, two point four (2.4) meters in height and/or two point four (2.4) meters in width (or: If any piece of CONTRACT GOODS exceeds the maximum weight and/or volume as specified in TECHNICAL AGREEMENT), SELLER shall submit the transport drawing and lifting procedure of the said item within three (3) months after signing of the CONTRACT, or in accordance with the timeframe given in the TECHNICAL AGREEMENT. This information shall be the basis for SELLER to deliver the item after confirmation by BUYER.

对于所有超大货物，如果任一件货物重量超过10公吨，长超过9米，高超过2.4米和/或宽超过2.4米（或者是任一件货物的最大重量或体积超过技术协议规定的数值），卖方在合同生效3个月内，或按照技术协议规定的时限，提供上述超限货物的运输图纸和吊装文件，在买方确认后，这些信息应作为交付货物的依据。

Meanwhile, SELLER shall, ninety (90) days before the date of delivery stipulated in the Contract, advice BUYER by email of the Contract No., name of Goods, quantity, value, number of packages, estimated gross weight, measurement (length, height, width).

同时在合同规定的交货日前90天，卖方应通过电子邮件通知买方合同号、货物名称、数量、价值、箱数、估计的毛重和尺寸（长、宽、高）。
