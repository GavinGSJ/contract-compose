名称_en: 'Requirements related to the main contract: Return and Buy-back Term'

对于工程中的剩余产品，买方有权对产品进行退货，退回的产品要按照合同规定产品售价的{{退货价格比例}}%计算，退回运费由买方承担。被回购的产品应保持交付时的原始状态，允许合理损耗，但不得影响其正常使用。若卖方未按约定回购材料，需向买方支付未回购材料总价值的{{回购违约金比例}}%作为违约金。因不可抗力导致回购无法履行，争议应通过双方协商解决，协商不成时提交合同签订地有管辖权的人民法院诉讼解决。

For the remaining products in the project, the Buyer has the right to return the products, and the returned products are to be calculated at {{退货价格比例}}% of the selling price of the products under the contract, and the return shipping costs are to be undertake by the Buyer. The repurchased products shall be kept in the original state at the time of delivery, and reasonable wear and tear is allowed, but shall not affect their normal use. If the seller fails to repurchase the materials as agreed, it shall pay {{回购违约金比例}}% of the total value of the materials not repurchased to the Buyer as liquidated damages. If the repurchase cannot be performed due to force majeure, the dispute shall be solved through consultation between the two parties, and if the consultation fails, the dispute shall be submitted to the People's Court with jurisdiction over the place where the contract was signed for litigation.
