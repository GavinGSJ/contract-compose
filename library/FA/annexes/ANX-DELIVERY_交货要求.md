为确保标的物快捷、顺利地送达项目建设现场，使我方提前做好接收准备，及时安排卸车，卖方在送货前做好以下工作：To ensure that the GOODS is delivered to the project construction site quickly and smoothly, so that we can prepare for receipt in advance and arrange for unloading in a timely manner, the SELLER shall do the following work before delivery:

+ 安排专车运输标的物，未经买方同意，不得采用配货、零担方式运输；运输车辆应务必做到人员、车辆证件齐全（如车辆保险等），否则将无法进入工程现场。Arrange dedicated vehicles to transport the GOODS, and without the BUYER's consent, it is not allowed to use distribution or less than truckload transportation methods; Transport vehicles must have complete personnel and vehicle documents (such as vehicle insurance), otherwise they will not be able to enter the construction site.

+ 发货前（{{发货通知天数}}）天请通过惠生一体化平台推送[发货清单]（具体要求见惠生一体化平台）。

  [{{发货通知天数}}] days before shipment, submit shipping notification through the Wison Integrated Platform (specific requirements can be found on the Wison Integrated Platform).

+ 标的物发货前请将送货车辆车号、司机联系方式、预计到达时间等通知到买方经办人。

  Inform the BUYER's agent of the vehicle plate number, driver's contact information, and estimated arrival time before shipping the GOODS.

+ 大宗材料在到达指定交货地点的前一天，卖方负责向买方再次确认到达具体时间。On the day before the arrival at the designated delivery location, the SELLER is responsible for reconfirming the specific arrival time with the BUYER.

+ 承运人应持有卖方填写的[送货（装箱）清单]一式贰份，交付标的物的同时交付买方。（如适用）The logistic agent shall hold two copies of the delivery (packing) list filled out by the SELLER and submit at the time of delivery of the GOODS to the BUYER. (If applicable)

+ 卖方应办理好标的物运送的全部手续，承担因运输纠纷导致的损失；出现货到现场不付运费不卸货的情况，如导致我方付费则加倍从合同价款中扣除。The SELLER shall handle all procedures for the transportation of the GOODS and bear the losses caused by transportation disputes; If there is a situation where the GOODS arrive at the site without paying the freight and are not unloaded, if it causes BUYER to pay, double the amount will be deducted from the contract price.

+ 标的物按我方指定送达惠生仓储库或工程项目现场。The GOODS shall be delivered to the Wison warehouse or project site as designated by BUYER.
