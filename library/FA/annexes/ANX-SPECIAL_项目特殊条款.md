The content of this {{附件_en:ANX-SPECIAL}} specifies the special requirements of the PROJECT. If there is any discrepancies or conflicts between {{附件_en:ANX-SPECIAL}} and other articles or attachments of this CONTRACT, {{附件_en:ANX-SPECIAL}} shall prevail and take precedence over the other articles or attachments, in accordance.

这{{附件:ANX-SPECIAL}}制定了项目特殊的要求。如果此附件与本合同的其他条款或附件有任何差异或冲突，应以{{附件:ANX-SPECIAL}}为准。{{附件:ANX-SPECIAL}}优先于其他条款或附件。

+ Seller shall provide any and all technical information as it may reasonably be requested by BUYER so that the latter is in a position to meet its regulatory obligations under the APPLICABLE LAW.

  卖方应根据买方的合理要求向买方提供相关的技术信息，以便买方能够履行适用法律规定的监管义务。

+ BUYER shall have the right to reject any part of the GOODS or rework which does not comply with any requirement(s) of the CONTRACT, including, but not limited to, faulty design, workmanship, services, welding, materials or equipment. Upon receiving notice of rejection SELLER shall immediately commence to reperform, repair or replace the defective part of the GOODS and shall carry out such inspections and/or tests on other parts of the GOODS as BUYER may require to ensure that there are no similar parts of the GOODS that fail to comply with the requirements of the CONTRACT, at all times at the expense of the CONTRACTOR. All associated expenses shall be borne by SELLER.

  买方有权拒收或要求返工不符合合同任一一条要求的任一部分的产品，包括但不限于有缺陷的设计、工艺、服务、焊接、材料或设备。在收到拒收或返工通知后，卖方应立即开始重做、修理或更换产品的缺陷部分，并应按照买方的要求对产品的其他部分进行检查和/或测试，以确保没有不符合本合同要求的类似缺陷，相关费用均由卖方承担。

+ Neither failure on the part of the BUYER to inspect the GOODS or witness or test or to discover DEFECTS nor failure to reject work performed by SELLER which is not in accordance with the CONTRACT shall relieve SELLER from any liability or obligation under the CONTRACT.

  买方未能检查、见证、测试及发现到缺陷，或未拒收卖方执行的不符合合同规定的工作，均不能免除卖方在合同项下的任何责任或义务。

+ In case of default on the part of the SELLER in carrying out its obligations under Reject or Rework Clause BUYER, having given prior notice to SELLER, shall be entitled to undertake (by itself or through a third party) the SELLER's responsibilities in this respect. And BUYER shall be entitled to recover from SELLER all costs reasonably incurred by BUYER in carrying out such responsibilities.

  如果卖方未能履行拒收或返工条款规定的义务，买方在事先通知卖方后，有权（自行或通过第三方）承担卖方在这方面的责任，同时买方有权向卖方收回买方在履行此类责任时产生的所有合理费用。

+ BUYER has the right to issue instructions to SELLER at any time to do any of the following:

  买方有权随时向卖方发出指令，以执行以下任何一项工作：

  1）Make any revision to the GOODS which may include additions, omissions, substitutions and changes in quality, form, character, kind, position, dimension, level or line and changes in any method of construction specified by the OWNER;

  根据业主提出的要求对产品进行的任何修订，其中可能包括质量、型式、性能、种类、位置、尺寸、标准或管线的添加、删除、替换和更改等。

  2）Revise elements of the GOODS already completed in accordance with the CONTRACT;

  修改已经按照合同完成的产品部件；

  3）After commencement of the GOODS, SELLER shall cooperated to accelerate the GOODS or any part thereof in order to recover all or part of any delay.

  在工作开始后为弥补全部或部分延迟，卖方应配合进行产品或其任何一个环节的赶工工作。

  4）Reprogramme the GOODS and reschedule its resource in order to complete the GOODS or any part thereof in accordance with any amendment to the SCHEDULE OF KEY DATES or the MASTER PROGRAMME the BUYER may require;

  根据买方的关键时间节点或控制流程调整产品整体或部分的需求或资源计划。

+ Title to any (a) GOODS and (b) goods, equipment, materials, and supplies provided by the SELLER shall vest in BUYER absolutely as soon as (i) delivered in accordance with the Incoterms specified in the CONTRACT, or (ii) when the payment has been made (whichever occurs earlier).

  卖方提供的任何(a)货物和(b)货物、设备、材料和交付物的所有权应在(i)按照合同规定的国际贸易术语交货后，或(ii)付款后（以先到时间为准）立即绝对归买方所有。

  Title to any equipment, materials and supplies provided by the SELLER which do not comply with the requirements of the CONTRACT and which are rejected by the BUYER and further reasonably demonstrated to be incurable and unrepairable by the SELLER within reasonable period, shall re-vest immediately in the SELLER. For the avoidance of any doubt, the foregoing—or the failure on the side of the BUYER to reject such equipment, materials and supplies—shall not be construed in the way that any obligation of the SELLER regarding such equipment, materials and supplies is waived or cease to exist.

  卖方提供的任何设备、材料和交付物如不符合合同要求、被买方拒绝接受，且在合理时间内被卖方声明无法修复和维修，则其所有权应立即重新归属卖方。为避免任何疑问，上述情形的设备、材料和交付物不能因为买方没有及时拒收而影响卖方的责任。

+ SELLER is responsible for supplying to BUYER any required documents on any Hazardous Substance pursuant to the APPLICABLE LAWS. SELLER is responsible for complying with all APPLICABLE LAWS regarding packaging, marking, and shipment of Hazardous Substance for delivery to the designated receiving location and shall indemnify and hold the BUYER harmless from and against any damages or loss which BUYER may incur due to such failure.

  卖方有责任根据适用法律的规定向买方提供任何有关危险物质的需求文件，卖方有责任遵守所有有关危险物包装、标记和装运的适用法律将危险物运送到指定的收货地点，卖方应保证买方免受危险物损害并赔偿买方因卖方未能遵守这些法律而可能遭受的任何损害或损失。

+ the performance testing will be conducted within one year after project delivery. If the performance fails to meet the specified standards within the 72-hour testing period,supplier will be responsible for the repairs性能测试将在项目交付后一年内进行，如果在72小时的测试期内性能未达到规定的标准，供应商将负责维修。
