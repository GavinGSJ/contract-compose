卖方需交付的文件资料除满足询价文件或技术协议的要求外，尚应满足以下要求：

The documents and materials that the SELLER needs to deliver should not only meet the requirements of the inquiry documents or Technical Agreements, but also meet the following requirements:

+ 字迹清楚，图样（章）清晰，图表整洁，签认盖章手续完备。质量文件必须电脑打印（签字除外），不应使用色带式打印机、水性墨打印机和热敏打印机，禁用易褪色的书写材料（红色墨水、纯蓝墨水、圆珠笔、复写纸、铅笔等）书写、绘制。Clear handwriting, clear design (stamp), neat charts, and complete signature and stamping procedures. Quality documents must be printed on a computer (except for signatures), and ribbon printers, water-based ink printers, and thermal printers should not be used. Writing and drawing on easily fading writing materials (red ink, pure blue ink, ballpoint pens, carbon paper, pencils, etc.) is prohibited.

+ 文字材料一般按A4（210mm×297mm）幅面编制，每页左边预留25mm的装订区域，图纸按照GB/T10609.3的要求折叠成A4幅面，与其他随机资料一起装订。Paper documents are generally prepared in A4 (210mm x 297mm) format, with a 25mm binding area reserved on the left side of each page. The drawings are folded into A4 format according to the requirements of GB/T10609.3 and bound together with other random materials.

+ 成册的文件资料必须要有资料目录、页码及到货清单，清单上的数量、型号、规格、材质等应与所供的货物相一致。A complete set of documents must have a table of contents, page numbers, and packing list. The quantity, model, specification, material, etc. on the list should be consistent with the supplied GOODS.

+ 质量检验文件每页正文应加盖制造厂质量检验专用章，无法提供原件的在产品质量证明文件复印件上加盖确认印章的延续性的质量证明文件，所盖印章不得模糊。The main text of each page of the quality inspection document should be stamped with the manufacturer's quality inspection special stamp. If the original copy cannot be provided, the quality certificate document that confirms the continuity of the stamp should be stamped on the copy of the product quality certificate document, and the stamp should not be blurred.

+ 交工资料用户名称为项目业主的名称，购件的产品合格证粘贴在设备随机资料内。过程文件或资料发送按[文件传送单]（如有）格式进行。并预先发送电子版，纸版采用快递方式或直接递交。The name of end user of the final documentation shall be the name of the project owner, and the product qualification certificate of the purchased parts is pasted in the GOODS. The process files or data shall be sent in the format of the [File Transfer Form] (if any). And send the electronic version in advance, the paper version will be sent by express delivery or directly submitted.

+ 交工资料工程名称、设备名称、设备位号等应按照设计图纸填写，包括质量证明文件包含的相关资料。Final documentation: The project name, equipment name, equipment tag number, etc. should be filled in according to the design drawings, including the relevant information contained in the quality certification documents.
