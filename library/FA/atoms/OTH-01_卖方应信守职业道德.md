## 卖方应信守职业道德，保证按已签署的《廉洁从业承诺书》执行，不以任何方式向买方相关人员进行商业贿赂或其他违纪行为，任何违反商业道德的行为都将导致合同终止或解除。如有此类事件发生，买方有权以书面形式通知卖方立即解除合同，解除合同的法律后果参照本合同{{附件:ANX-INTEGRITY}}《廉洁从业承诺书》中有关条款执行。

The SELLER shall abide by professional ethics, ensure to execute in accordance with the signed Integrity Commitment, and shall not engage in any commercial bribery or other disciplinary behavior towards the BUYER's relevant personnel in any way. Any behavior that violates commercial ethics will result in the termination or termination of the contract. If such events occur, the BUYER has the right to notify the SELLER in writing to immediately terminate the contract. The legal consequences of terminating the contract shall refer to {{附件_en:ANX-INTEGRITY}} Undertaking of Honest Conduct.
