## 本合同为框架合同，买方可依据本合同向卖方下发采购订单（简称“订单”），该等订单受本合同条款约束。但本合同不构成买方向卖方的采购承诺，也不构成买方向卖方的独家采购承诺，买方在任何情况下都没有义务发出采购订单，也无义务做出任何有关最低订单数量的承诺。

This Contract is a framework contract, BUYER may place Purchase Orders (hereinafter referred to as the "Order") with the SELLER under this CONTRACT, and such orders shall be subject to the terms of this CONTRACT. This Contract does not constitute a procurement commitment or an exclusive purchase commitment from BUYER to SELLER. BUYER shall not be obliged to issue Orders under any circumstances, nor shall it be obliged to make any commitment regarding minimum order quantities.
