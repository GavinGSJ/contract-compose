### Project Manager 项目经理

Mr. \*\*\*  has been assigned as Seller's Project Manager for the execution of this Purchase Order, including the issue of notices and all communications. The Project Manager shall be responsible for the implementation and management of all activities required for successful completion of the work.  Seller shall at all times insure that the Project Manager is provided with resources that are appropriate and adequate to ensure completion in accordance with Buyer’s requirements, by the agreed upon date.

\*\*\*先生已被任命为卖方的项目经理以执行采购订单，包括通知发布和所有交流通信。项目经理将负责顺利竣工所要求的所有行为的执行和管理。卖方应随时确保项目经理有适当和充足的资源以保证在合同规定日期下根据买方要求完成此合同。
