## 质量责任：在约定的质量保证期内，卖方应对由于制造工艺或材料的缺陷等原因而造成的质量问题负责。卖方应在收到买方通知48小时内赶到现场，免费负责修理或更换有缺陷的货物，且不得因此影响买方的工程进度；若卖方未能在买方指定的期限内予以修理或更换的，或修理/更换不能满足合同或项目要求的，买方有权根据项目实际情况请第三方完成该工作，所发生所有费用和造成的损失由卖方承担，且买方有权从卖方质保金（若有）或任何一笔合同款中扣除，不足部分由卖方补足。

Quality Responsibility: During the agreed Warranty period, the SELLER shall be responsible for any quality problems caused by defects in manufacturing processes or materials. The SELLER shall arrive at the site within 48 hours of receiving the BUYER's notice, be responsible for repairing or replacing defective GOODS free of charge, and shall not affect the BUYER's engineering progress as a result; If the SELLER fails to repair or replace within the time limit specified by the BUYER, or if the repair/replacement cannot meet the requirements of the contract or project, the BUYER has the right to request a third party to complete the work according to the actual situation of the project. All expenses and losses incurred shall be borne by the SELLER, and the BUYER has the right to deduct from the SELLER's warranty deposit (if any) or any contract payment, and the shortfall shall be made up by the SELLER.
