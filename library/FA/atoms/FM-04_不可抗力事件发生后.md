## 不可抗力事件发生后，受影响一方应采取有效措施，以最短的时间和最大努力将不可抗力造成的损失降到最低程度。当不再受不可抗力影响时，应通知另一方，并在最短的时间内恢复受不可抗力影响的工作。

After the occurrence of force majeure events, the affected party shall take effective measures to minimize the losses caused by force majeure in the shortest possible time and with the greatest effort. When no longer affected by force majeure, the other party shall be notified and work affected by force majeure shall be resumed as soon as possible.
