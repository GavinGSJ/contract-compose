## 检验、验收要求Inspection and Acceptance Requirements

- 对于重要、关键物资，卖方应提供本项目负责人联系方式和执行人员名单、生产网络计划（进度计划）、质量检验计划（ITP计划），在合同生效后第（{{计划提交天数}}）日历日内提交买方审核确认。

  For important and critical materials, the SELLER shall provide the contact information of the project manager and the list of executing personnel, production plan (schedule plan), inspection and test plan (ITP plan), and submit them to the BUYER for review and confirmation within ({{计划提交天数}}) calendar days after the contract takes effect；

- 合同履行期间，卖方在每月25日前按时给买方提供一份真实合同执行状态和进度报告During the performance of the contract, the SELLER shall provide the BUYER with a genuine contract execution status and progress report on time before the 25th of each month；

- 卖方所有报送买方的报告均需项目负责人签字，买方将根据卖方提供的报告，适时对质量、进度状况跟踪检查。

  All reports submitted by the SELLER to the BUYER must be signed by the project manager. The BUYER will track and inspect the quality and progress status in a timely manner based on the reports provided by the SELLER；

- 卖方应严格执行《技术协议》约定的分包商名单，如有变动，应征得买方书面同意，否则买方有权解除合同，并要求卖方返还买方已经支付的款项，若给买方造成损失的，则卖方须承担赔偿责任，赔偿买方因此而遭受的经济损失，该损失的范围包括但不限于直接损失、可得利益及第三方索赔等间接损失、重新订货的差价、为追究卖方违约责任而支付的律师费用、诉讼仲裁费用、调查取证费用以及其他合理的费用支出等The SELLER shall strictly comply with the list of subcontractors specified in the Technical Agreement. If there are any changes, the BUYER's written consent shall be obtained. Otherwise, the BUYER has the right to terminate the contract and request the SELLER to return the payment already made by the BUYER. If any losses are caused to the BUYER, the SELLER shall bear the compensation responsibility and compensate for the economic losses suffered by the BUYER as a result. The scope of such losses includes but is not limited to direct losses, obtainable benefits, and indirect losses such as third-party claims, price differences for reordering, lawyer fees paid to pursue the SELLER's breach of contract, litigation and arbitration fees, investigation and evidence fees, and other reasonable expenses；

- 根据需要，买方有权要求卖方提供本合同外购、外协件制造商资质、业绩及联系方式。买方将根据外购、外协件重要程度与卖方共同确认外购、外协件的制造资格，但买方的确认不能免除卖方对外协件的质量责任。

  As needed, the BUYER has the right to request the SELLER to provide the qualifications, performance, and contact information of manufacturers of purchased and outsourced parts under this CONTRACT. The BUYER will jointly confirm the manufacturing qualifications of the purchased and outsourced parts with the SELLER based on their criticality, but the BUYER's confirmation cannot exempt the SELLER from the quality responsibility of the outsourced parts；

- 按照质量检验计划（ITP计划），买方参与中间检验、出厂检验时，卖方应提前7天发出通知；经买方在卖方制造工厂检验合格并签署[产品检验放行单]后，买方方可进行标的物的包装和发运准备。

  According to the Inspection and Test Plan (ITP plan), when the BUYER participates in intermediate and factory inspections, the SELLER should provide a notice of 7 days in advance; After the BUYER passes the inspection at the SELLER's manufacturing factory and signs the [Inspection Release Note], the BUYER can proceed with the packaging and shipping preparation of the GOODS；

- 买方及其委托的监造人员有权进入卖方为本合同标的物制作场所及分包商工厂，进行与本合同相关的活动。卖方应为买方及其委托的监造人员在入厂检验时提供工作的支持和生活的便利。

  The BUYER and its authorized supervisors have the right to enter the SELLER's production site and subcontractor's factory for the GOODS of this CONTRACT to carry out activities related to this CONTRACT. The SELLER shall provide work support and convenience for the BUYER and its authorized supervision personnel during the on-site inspection；

- 买方安排监检人员赴卖方处检验，费用由买方负担，但卖方需提供工作、生活便利The BUYER arranges for inspection personnel to go to the SELLER's place for inspection, and the cost shall be borne by the BUYER, but the SELLER shall provide convenience for work and life；

- 买方参与的检验、验收或委托的第三方监造，不免除卖方应承担的质量责任；

  The inspection, acceptance, or third-party supervision commissioned by the BUYER shall not exempt the SELLER from its quality responsibility;

- 标的物的签收不等于买方认可和免除卖方的产品质量责任；若无特殊原因，买方收货后10日历日内组织验收，并提前2个日历日通知卖方参加；卖方逾期未参加验收的，视为同意买方单方面进行验收并接受验收结果；

  The acceptance of the GOODS does not mean that the BUYER recognizes and exempts the SELLER from product quality responsibility; If there are no special reasons, the BUYER shall organize acceptance within 10 calendar days after receiving the GOODS, and notify the SELLER to participate 2 calendar days in advance; If the SELLER fails to participate in the acceptance within the specified time, it shall be deemed that they agree to the BUYER's unilateral acceptance decision and accept the acceptance result;

- 验收结果与合同约定不符的，买方应在验收后5个日历日内书面通知卖方，并有权要求卖方在（{{整改天数}}）个日历日内对不合格标的物进行整改、修理、重做/或更换、或降质折价等，卖方有义务按照合同和《技术协议》的要求，采取相关补救措施，直至符合合同和《技术协议》约定；对于整改、修理、重做/或更换、或降质折价等所造成的延迟应按照{{ref:LIA-01}}及{{ref:LIA-02}}进行赔偿；

  If the acceptance test result is inconsistent with the contract agreement, the BUYER shall notify the SELLER in writing within 5 calendar days after acceptance, and has the right to request the SELLER to rectify, repair, redo/or replace the unqualified GOODS within ({{整改天数}}) calendar days, or reduce the quality or price. The SELLER is obliged to take relevant remedial measures in accordance with the requirements of the contract and the Technical Agreement until it meets the provisions of the contract and the Technical Agreement; Compensation shall be made in accordance with {{ref:LIA-01}} and {{ref:LIA-02}} for delays caused by rectification, repair, redoing/or replacement, or degradation or discount;

- 标的物缺陷不能补救或改正，则买方有权拒收，卖方必须以合适的标的物进行更换以满足本合同的要求，应在最短（{{更换天数}}）个日历日内完成更换工作，否则买方有权解除本合同并重新订货，解除合同的法律后果同第{{ref:LIA-03}}条规定。

  If the defects of the GOODS cannot be remedied or corrected, the BUYER has the right to reject, and the SELLER must replace it with a suitable one to meet the requirements of this CONTRACT. The replacement work should be completed within the shortest ({{更换天数}}) calendar days. Otherwise, the BUYER has the right to terminate this CONTRACT and reorder. The legal consequences of terminating the contract are the same as those stipulated in Article {{ref:LIA-03}}.
