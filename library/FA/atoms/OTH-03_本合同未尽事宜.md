## 本合同未尽事宜，双方协商签署补充协议，若补充协议与本合同有不一致的，以补充协议为准。卖方应接受买方交付标的物前所有的合理变更要求，若对本合同条款、技术协议有任何变更，必须经原签署各方授权代表书面签字确认，方可生效。

Matters not covered in this CONTRACT shall be supplemented by a supplementary agreement signed by both parties through negotiation. In case of any inconsistency between the supplementary agreement and this CONTRACT, the supplementary agreement shall prevail. The SELLER shall accept all reasonable changes requested by the BUYER before delivering the GOODS. If there are any changes to the terms of this CONTRACT or Technical Agreement, they must be confirmed in writing by the authorized representatives of the original signing parties before they can take effect.
