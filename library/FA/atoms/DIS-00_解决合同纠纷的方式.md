# 解决合同纠纷的方式Ways to Resolve Contract Disputes

合同双方因执行本合同所发生的或与本合同有关的一切争议和纠纷，首先应通过友好协商解决：如协商不成，则任何一方均可按（{{争议解决方式}}）执行。All disputes and controversies arising from or in connection with the execution of this CONTRACT between the two parties shall first be resolved through friendly consultation. If consultation fails, either party may proceed in accordance with ({{争议解决方式}}) below:

- 向合同签订地有管辖权的人民法院提起诉讼。Bring a lawsuit to the people's court with jurisdiction over the place where the contract was signed.

- 提交上海仲裁委员会仲裁，仲裁地点为上海，仲裁裁决对双方均具有约束力。Submit to the Shanghai Arbitration Commission for arbitration, with the arbitration venue in Shanghai. The arbitration result shall be binding on both parties.
