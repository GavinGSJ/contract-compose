## 卖方服务人员完成现场服务工作后，须填写[供应商现场服务确认单]，并经买方施工专业工程师、施工经理确认。

After the SELLER's service personnel complete the on-site service work, they must fill out the [Supplier's on-site service confirmation form] and obtain confirmation from the BUYER's construction professional engineer and construction manager.
