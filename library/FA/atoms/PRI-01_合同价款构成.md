以上合同价款为卖方为履行本合同所需之全部费用，包括但不限于以下费用：

The above contract price represents all expenses required by the SELLER to perform this CONTRACT, including but not limited to the following expenses:

- 合同标的物的设计费、图纸及技术资料费Design fee, drawing and technical data fee for the GOODS of the contract；

- 形成标的物所需的有关加工、检验和试验费，以及各种管理费、税费和利润等The processing, inspection, and testing fees required to form the GOODS, as well as various management fees, taxes, and fees；

- 两年备件费（清单详见附件五《技术协议》）Two year spare parts cost (see Annex 5 "Technical Agreement" for a detailed list)

- 卖方工厂至买方施工现场的所有包装费、标识费、运输费、装车费、保险费等All packaging fees, labeling fees, transportation fees, loading fees, insurance fees, etc. from the SELLER's factory to the BUYER's construction site；

- 到买方现场指导安装、调试费及培训服务费On site guidance, installation, debugging fees, and training service fees for the BUYER；

- 附件五《技术协议》规定的其它工作范围内发生的全部费用，买方不再向卖方支付本合同价格之外的任何其它费用All expenses incurred within the scope of supply by other work specified in Annex 5 of the Technical Agreement shall not be paid by the BUYER to the SELLER for any other expenses beyond the price of this CONTRACT；

- 本合同价格为含税总价，如遇国家税率调整，税额部分将按新税率进行相应调整和退补。The price of this CONTRACT is the total price including tax. In case of any national tax rate adjustment, the tax amount will be adjusted and refunded according to the new tax rate

  除上述合同价款外，买方无需支付任何其他费用。Apart from the above contract price, the BUYER is not required to pay any other fees
