## 包装的标识由卖方负责，符合附件五《技术协议》及相关包装标准规范的要求。该标识应包含买方合同号、产品名称、收货人地址、发货起迄地、发货人和发货单位。The identification of packaging shall be the responsibility of the SELLER, in accordance with the requirements of Annex 5 Technical Agreement and relevant packaging standards and specifications. The identification should include the BUYER's contract number, product name, recipient's address, origin and destination of shipment, shipping agent, and consignor.
