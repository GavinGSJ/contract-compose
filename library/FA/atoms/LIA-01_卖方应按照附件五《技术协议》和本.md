## 卖方应按照{{附件:ANX-TA}}《技术协议》和本合同规定的时间节点交付相关技术文件，因未按期交付影响设计进度的，按合同总价的（{{文件延误违约金比例}}）% /周向买方支付违约金，交付迟延的天数不满壹周的按照壹周计算。买方有权从任何一笔应付合同款中扣除该违约金。The SELLER shall deliver the relevant technical documentation in accordance with {{附件_en:ANX-TA}} "Technical Agreement" and the time nodes specified in this CONTRACT. If the failure to deliver on time affects the design progress, a penalty of ({{文件延误违约金比例}})% of the total contract price per week shall be paid to the BUYER. If the delivery is delayed for less than one week, it shall be calculated as one week. The BUYER has the right to deduct the penalty from any payable contract payment.
