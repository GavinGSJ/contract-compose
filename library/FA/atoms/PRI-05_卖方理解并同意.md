## 卖方理解并同意，尽管有上述{{ref:PRI-02}}款约定，买方非合同标的物最终用户或项目业主，买方仅在收到最终用户或项目业主支付的对应采购款项后，才有义务向卖方支付相应的合同款项。买方向卖方支付本合同项下的价款可能会受到合同标的物最终用户或项目业主对买方资金支付的实际情况的影响，此种影响将不能成为卖方延迟或暂缓履行合同项下义务的理由。若买方付款因最终用户或项目业主对买方资金支付而受到影响，不构成买方在本合同项下的违约，卖方不得向买方主张索赔。

The SELLER understands and agrees that, despite the provisions of clause {{ref:PRI-02}} above, if the BUYER is not the end user or project owner of the GOODS of the contract, the BUYER is only obligated to pay the corresponding contract amount to the SELLER after receiving the corresponding purchase payment from the end user or project owner. The payment of the price by the BUYER to the SELLER under this CONTRACT may be affected by the actual situation of the payment of funds by the end user or project owner of the contract GOODS to the BUYER, and such influence shall not be a reason for the SELLER to delay or suspend the performance of obligations under the contract. If the BUYER's payment is affected by the payment of funds by the end user or project owner to the BUYER, it does not constitute a breach of contract by the BUYER under this CONTRACT, and the SELLER shall not claim compensation from the BUYER.
