## 若卖方违反上述保密义务，应承担违约责任，支付违约金给买方，违约金金额为合同总额的{{泄密违约金比例}}%，若违约金不能弥补买方因此而遭受的实际损失的，则以实际损失为准，该实际损失包括直接损失、预期利益的损失、买方为追究卖方的违约责任和/或为防止/制止泄密而支付的律师费用、诉讼仲裁费用、调查取证费用以及其他合理的费用支出等。

If the SELLER violates the above confidentiality obligations, they shall bear the liability for breach of contract and pay a penalty to the BUYER, which is 10% of the total contract amount. If the penalty cannot compensate for the actual losses suffered by the BUYER as a result, the actual losses shall prevail. The actual losses include direct losses, loss of expected benefits, legal fees paid by the BUYER to pursue the SELLER's breach of contract liability and/or to prevent/stop leakage, litigation and arbitration fees, investigation and evidence fees, and other reasonable expenses, etc.
