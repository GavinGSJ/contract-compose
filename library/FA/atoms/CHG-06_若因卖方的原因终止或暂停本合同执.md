## 若因卖方的原因终止或暂停本合同执行的，除本合同第{{ref:LIA-03}}条规定的违约责任外，卖方还应承担买方由此产生的所有损失费用，包括但不仅限于可得利益及第三方索赔、重新签订合同的差价、由此产生的赶工费和导致项目延期的罚款、违约金等。If the execution of this CONTRACT is terminated or suspended due to the SELLER's reasons, in addition to the breach of contract liability stipulated in Article {{ref:LIA-03}} of this CONTRACT, the SELLER shall also bear all losses and expenses incurred by the BUYER as a result, including but not limited to benefits and third-party claims, price differences in re-signing the contract, expediting costs incurred as a result, fines for project delays, liquidated damages, etc.
