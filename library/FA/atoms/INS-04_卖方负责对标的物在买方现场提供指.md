## 卖方负责对标的物在买方现场提供指导安装与调试，时间、人次执行《技术协议》的要求,必须遵守买方的HSE管理要求，佩带劳动防护用品，遵从买方的统一管理。现场指导安装与调试人员必须是有经验、能胜任相关职责的技术人员或技师。

The SELLER is responsible for providing guidance, installation, and commissioning of the GOODS at the BUYER's site. The time and number of personnel shall comply with the requirements of the Technical Agreement, and must comply with the BUYER's HSE management requirements, wear personal protection equipment, and comply with the instruction from BUYER's management. On site guidance for installation and commissioning personnel must be experienced and competent technical personnel or technicians with relevant expertise.
