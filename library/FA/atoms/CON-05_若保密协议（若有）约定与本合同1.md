## 若保密协议（若有）约定与本合同第{{ref:CON-00}}条约定不一致，以较高义务和责任为准。

If there is any inconsistency between the confidentiality agreement (if any) and the provisions of Article {{ref:CON-00}} of this CONTRACT, the higher obligations and responsibilities shall prevail.
