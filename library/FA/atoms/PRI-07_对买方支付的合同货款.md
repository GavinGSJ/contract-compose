## 对买方支付的合同货款，卖方保证只用于为了完成本合同内容的工作中，买方有权要求卖方提供相应财务数据或财务凭证等以作核实，若卖方将买方支付的合同货款用于非本合同约定的目的，买方有权追回已付合同款项并解除合同，且相关损失由卖方承担。The SELLER guarantees that the contract payment made by the BUYER will only be used for the purpose of completing the work specified in this CONTRACT. The BUYER has the right to request the SELLER to provide corresponding financial data or vouchers for verification. If the SELLER uses the contract payment made by the BUYER for purposes other than those specified in this CONTRACT, the BUYER has the right to recover the paid contract payment and terminate the contract, and the related losses shall be borne by the SELLER.
