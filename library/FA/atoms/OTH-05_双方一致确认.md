## 双方一致确认，有关本合同的相关书面通知以及其它法律文件的送达地址与送达的联系人以合同扉页所记载的地址以及委托代理人为准，若一方有所变更的，则应提前5个日历日发送通知给对方，若发生变更方未能在前述期限内发送通知给对方的，则该变更方的送达地址以及联系人以合同扉页所记载的为准。本合同一式2份，买方执1份，卖方执1份，自双方法定代表人或法人授权代理人签字加盖合同专用章或公章之日起生效。Both parties agree that the delivery addresses and contact persons for relevant written notices and other legal documents related to this CONTRACT shall be based on the addresses recorded on the contract cover page and the authorized representatives. If one party changes, they shall send a notice to the other party 5 calendar days in advance. If the changing party fails to send a notice to the other party within the aforementioned period, the delivery address and contact person of the changing party shall be based on the address recorded on the contract cover page. This contract is made in two original copies, with the BUYER holding one copy and the SELLER holding the other one. It shall come into effect from the date of signing and affixing the contract stamp or company by the legal representatives or authorized agents of both parties.
