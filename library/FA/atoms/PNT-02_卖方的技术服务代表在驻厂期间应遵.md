## 卖方的技术服务代表在驻厂期间应遵守买方的各种规章制度，并且卖方应确保其穿着个人防护用品并遵守现场安全规章。如果报验时不具备必要的安全条件，卖方技术服务人员有权拒绝报验。卖方应准备合适的检测设备，配合船厂完成检验工作。

SELLER’s technical service representative(s) shall abide by the BUYER’s regulations whilst working in the shipyard and SELLER shall ensure that they wear the proper personal protection equipment and observe safety regulations on site. The technical service representative(s) may refuse to carry out any inspection work if the proper safety procedures are not in place. SELLER shall prepare appropriate equipment to assist YARD to accomplish the detection job.
