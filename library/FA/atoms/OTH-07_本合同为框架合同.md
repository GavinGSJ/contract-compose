## 本合同为框架合同，本项目后续采购订单将按此框架合同执行；如有未在框架合同清单的规格型号，则参照本框架合同清单类似规格型号物料价格做核算增补；单价核算方式为：相近项的数量单价/相近项的理论单重\*新规格的理论单重，如对理论单重有异议，则卖方与买方设计进行澄清，按照双方最终认可的单重执行。此框架合同执行至项目结束（以买方通知为准），未尽事宜，由双方友好协商解决。This is a framework contract, and the subsequent purchase order for this project will be executed in accordance with this framework contract. If there are specifications and models that are not included in the framework contract list, the material prices of similar specifications and models in this framework contract list shall be supplemented for accounting purposes. The unit price calculation method is: the quantity unit price of similar items/the theoretical unit weight of similar items \* the theoretical unit weight of new specifications. If there is any objection to the theoretical single weight. The seller shall clarify with the design personnel of the buyer and execute it according to the final agreed single weight by both parties. This framework contract will be executed until the end of the project (subject to the notification by the buyer). Any matters not covered in this contract shall be resolved through friendly negotiation between both parties.
