## 如因买方原因终止合同，根据核实的进度状态及对应价格，买方应向卖方支付截至终止之日已经实际交付并经买方验收合格的货物所对应的价款。若双方达成一致对应货物所有权仍归卖方，其对应的货值在合同终止结算中进行抵扣。

If the contract is terminated due to the BUYER's reasons, based on the verified progress status and corresponding price, the BUYER shall pay the SELLER the price corresponding to the GOODS that have been actually delivered and accepted by the BUYER as of the date of termination. If both parties reach an agreement that the ownership of the corresponding GOODS still belongs to the SELLER, the corresponding value of the GOODS shall be deducted in the settlement upon termination of the contract.
