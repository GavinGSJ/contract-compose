## 因包装不符合标准或约定，造成标的物毁损灭失或其他后果的，由卖方承担相应责任。If the packaging does not meet the standards or agreements, resulting in damage or loss of the GOODS or other consequences, the SELLER shall bear corresponding responsibilities
