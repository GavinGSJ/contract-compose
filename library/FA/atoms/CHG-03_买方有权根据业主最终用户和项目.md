## 买方有权根据业主/最终用户和项目的具体情况随时解除本合同，且不承担违约责任。卖方在收到提前解除的书面通知后，应立即终止履行本合同，并应在（{{进度报告天数}}）天内提供详细的生产进度报告，报告内容须包含但不仅限于以下文件

The BUYER has the right to terminate this CONTRACT at any time based on the specific situation of the owner/end user and the project, and shall not be liable for breach of contract. The SELLER shall immediately terminate the performance of this CONTRACT upon receiving written notice of early termination, and shall provide a detailed production progress report within ({{进度报告天数}}) days, which shall include but not be limited to the following documents:

- 已完成成品清单及价格Completed finished product list and price

- 半成品清单及价格List and price of semi-finished products

- 未开工生产的原材料Raw materials that have not yet started production

- 以上所列物资的对应照片Corresponding photos of the materials listed above

- 买方要求的其他文件Other documents requested by the BUYER
