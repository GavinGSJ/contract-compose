## 对标的物的检验、验收按照本合同《技术协议》和审批的【设备检验、试验计划（ITP）】执行。验收方式为（{{验收方式}}）或在设备预检会上确定

The inspection and acceptance of the GOODS shall be carried out in accordance with the Technical Agreement of this CONTRACT and the approved Equipment Inspection and Test Plan (ITP). The acceptance method is ({{验收方式}}) or determined at the equipment pre inspection meeting：

- 由买方或委托第三方进行全程驻厂监造Full on-site supervision by the BUYER or commissioned third party；

- 由买方（业主根据需要）参加的中间过程和出厂检验放行Intermediate process and factory inspection release attended by the BUYER (owner as needed)；

- 由买方（业主根据需要）参加的出厂检验放行Factory inspection release attended by the BUYER (owner as needed)；

- 货到买方指定地点后的现场验收On site acceptance after the GOODS arrive at the BUYER's designated location；
