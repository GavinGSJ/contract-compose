## 当不可抗力发生时，遭受不可抗力方应尽可能将不可抗力事件的发生在最短的时间内通知另一方，并在其后10个日历日内向另一方发出有关部门出具的证明书或其它为公众认可的资料作为证明。

When force majeure occurs, the party affected by the force majeure shall notify the other party of the occurrence of the force majeure event as soon as possible, and within 10 calendar days thereafter, issue a certificate issued by relevant departments or other publicly recognized materials to the other party as proof.
