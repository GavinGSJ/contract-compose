## 如果卖方有责任向买方支付任何违约赔偿金，则卖方应在收到买方的索赔通知后10天内通过电汇支付，否则买方有权从将支付给卖方的任何款项中扣除该金额，或尽可能从履约保函、质保保函或质保金中扣除违约赔偿金。如果通过上述方式仍有任何无法收回的款项，买方有权按照本合同约定的争议解决方式向卖方要求赔偿

If the SELLER is responsible for paying any liquidated damages to the BUYER, the SELLER shall make the payment by wire transfer within 10 days after receiving the BUYER's claim notice. Otherwise, the BUYER has the right to deduct the amount from any payment to be made to the SELLER, or deduct the liquidated damages as much as possible from the performance bond, warranty bond, or warranty deposit. If there is still any amount that cannot be recovered through the above methods, the BUYER has the right to demand compensation from the SELLER in accordance with the dispute resolution method stipulated in this CONTRACT.
