## 本合同下，若卖方按照合同约定应支付的违约金不足以弥补买方的实际损失的，则卖方之赔偿范围应以买方的实际损失为准。该实际损失包括但不限于直接损失、可得利益及第三方索赔等间接损失、重新订货的差价、为追究卖方违约责任而支付的律师费用、诉讼仲裁费用、调查取证费用以及其他实际的费用支出等

Under this CONTRACT, if the liquidated damages payable by the SELLER according to the contract are insufficient to compensate for the actual losses of the BUYER, the SELLER's compensation scope shall be based on the actual losses of the BUYER. The actual losses include but are not limited to direct losses, financial losses, and indirect losses such as third-party claims, price differences for reordering, legal fees paid to pursue the SELLER's breach of contract, litigation and arbitration fees, investigation and evidence collection fees, and other actual expenses.
