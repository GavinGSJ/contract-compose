## 若需要卖方或者其分包商厂家的服务工程师到场时，但卖方无法到现场时，买方有权按照操作手册或说明书进行施工或维修，但不免除卖方在合同项下包括第{{ref:QUA-04}}条所述的质保期内应承担的责任。If SELLER or its SUBCONTRACTOR’s service engineers are unable to attend at any WORKS site as required, BUYER has the right to construction, and/or repair in accordance with the manufacturer’s manual or instructions, and such performance shall not affect the SELLER’s warranty responsibility and shall not relieve SELLER of any obligations under this CONTRACT including any warranty obligations in accordance with Clause {{ref:QUA-04}}.
