## 卖方应当按照保密协议（若有）对买方提供的资料（包括但不限于技术方案、工程设计、技术图纸、技术指标、价格信息、经营信息、项目信息及其它商业秘密等，无论是有形或无形的）保守秘密。未经买方书面同意，卖方不得向任何第三方（包括但不限于卖方无须知晓保密信息的雇员、管理人员或委托代理等）泄露该商业秘密的全部或部分内容，法律法规另有规定的除外。本保密义务应在本合同期满、解除或终止后仍然有效，卖方的保密义务不受本合同效力影响。

The SELLER shall keep confidential the information provided by the BUYER (including but not limited to technical solutions, engineering designs, technical drawings, technical indicators, price information, business information, project information, and other trade secrets, whether tangible or intangible) in accordance with the confidentiality agreement (if any). Without the written consent of the BUYER, the SELLER shall not disclose all or part of the contents of the trade secret to any third party (including but not limited to employees, managers, or authorized agents who are not required to know confidential information), except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations. This confidentiality obligation shall remain valid even after the expiration, termination, or termination of this CONTRACT, and the confidentiality obligation of the SELLER shall not be affected by the validity of this CONTRACT.
