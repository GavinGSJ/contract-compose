## 质量保证 Quality Guarantee

- 卖方应保证提供的标的物符合本合同附件五《技术协议》和合同约定质量要求，标的物是全新的、未使用过的、技术成熟的，并在各个方面符合本合同规定的质量、规格和性能要求。不得提供国家、行业、国际、国际协会等禁止使用、明令淘汰、限期禁止使用的设备或材料。

  The SELLER shall ensure that the provided GOODS meets the quality requirements stipulated in Annex 5 of this CONTRACT, including the Technical Agreement and the contract. The GOODS is brand new, unused, technologically mature, and meets the quality, specifications, and performance requirements stipulated in this CONTRACT in all aspects. Do not provide equipment or materials that are prohibited, explicitly phased out, or prohibited for use within a specified period by countries, industries, international organizations, or international associations.

- 质量保证期：{{选项:质保期}}若法律规定的质保期或产品出厂质保证书记载的质保期长于本合同约定的质保期，则以法律规定的质保期或产品出厂质保证书记载的质保期为准。若在质量保证期间出现任何质量问题，则卖方应按照第{{ref:QUA-03}}条款进行修理或更换，标的物的质量保证期自修理或更换合格之日重新起算。

  Warranty period: {{选项:质保期_en}} If the warranty period specified by law or the warranty period recorded in the product's factory warranty certificate is longer than the warranty period specified in this CONTRACT, the warranty period specified by law or the warranty period recorded in the product's factory warranty certificate shall prevail. If any quality problems occur during the Warranty period, the SELLER shall repair or replace them in accordance with clause {{ref:QUA-03}}, and the Warranty period of the GOODS shall be recalculated from the date of qualified repair or replacement.

- 涂料供应商应为防护涂料系统提供担保，质保期应为项目运行开始后{{涂层质保月数}}个月，具体要求以技术协议为准。

  The coating manufacturer shall provide a warranty for the applied protective coating system, shall be {{涂层质保月数}} months from onshore start-up, the Technical Agreement will prevail.

- 适用于修复缺陷设备、部件和工艺的任何供货的质保期应从维修或更换之日起再延长12个月，或至第{{ref:QUA-04}} b) 条规定的质保期结束为止，以较长者为准。

  The warranty period applicable to any SUPPLY to rectify defective equipment, parts and workmanship shall be for a further 12 months from the date of repair or replacement or until the end of the warranty period stated in Article {{ref:QUA-04}} b), whichever is longer.

  如果有缺陷的货物或者部件因维修或者更换需要自项目装置内拆除，卖方应支付给买方因拆除缺陷货物或者部件以及重新安装维修或者更换部件所产生的合理费用。此外，如果维修或者更换涉及将缺陷货物或者部件返回卖方工厂或其他指定地点进行的，运输缺陷货物或部件以及返回相应货物或部件而对买方所产生的运输及保险费用、税费等（包括清关费用、关税）应由卖方承担。在货物无法使用这段期间而导致买方的直接损失，包括但不限于窝工费，财务损失等将由卖方承担。

  If it is necessary for any defective part(s) of the CONTRACT GOODS to be removed from the PROJECT for repair or replacement, the SELLER shall be liable for the BUYER’s reasonable costs of removing the defective part(s) and re-installing repaired or replacement part(s). Furthermore, where repair or replacement involves the return of defective part(s) to the SELLER’s premises or any to other place for repair, the carriage and insurance costs of shipment to the relevant place of repair and returning repaired or replacement part(s) duties to the BUYER, including customs clearance fees, customs duties, shall be borne by the SELLER. All other direct costs suffered by BUYER, including but not limited to the standby cost, financial damage during the unavailability of SUPPLY shall be borne by the SELLER, too.

- 对于工程中的剩余产品，在外观保存完好的情况下买方有权对产品质保期剩余在半年内的产品进行退货，退回的产品要按照合同规定产品售价计算，退回运费由买方承担（Resist 78、Tankguard 412、Marathons 550、Jotatemp 1000、Hardtop XP产品不可退货）。（不适用）

  For the remaining products in the project, the buyer has the right to return the products with good appearance condition and of which remained shelf life are longer than 6 months. The price of returned products shall be calculated according to the price specified in the contract, and the return shipping cost shall be paid by the buyer (Resist 78, Tankguard 412, Marathons 550, Jotatemp 1000, Hardtop XP cannot be returned).（N/A）
