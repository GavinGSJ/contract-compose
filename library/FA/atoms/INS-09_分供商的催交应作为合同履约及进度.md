## 分供商的催交应作为合同履约及进度报告的一部分，在图纸和分包合同释放后，卖方应派专职人员至少每两周至原材料或部件工厂进行催交并查看进度，并提供买方附带照片的报告，不允许只远程催交，且随时响应与我司派驻的催交人员现场协作。Expedition for sub-suppliers shall be part of the contract performance and progress report, in the aspect of drawings and purchase order released and approved, the SELLER shall deploy a full time personnel bi-weekly at least to the raw materials or parts factory to expedite the delivery and review the progress by providing the report with photo to BUYER, not only remote expediting and always cooperate with our expeditor or resident expeditor at the place of work.
