## Technical instructions by SELLER's PERSONNEL shall be submitted in written form.

卖方人员的技术说明应以书面的形式提交。

SELLER must ensure that their PERSONNEL has been properly insured with accident insurance covering at minimum CNY one million per occurrence prior to the mobilization of the PERSONNEL. Such PERSONNEL must fully adhere to the SITE HSE regulations.

卖方必须确保其人员在调动前已适当投保了意外事故险，每起事故的保险金额至少为100万元人民币。这些人员必须完全遵守现场的HSE管理制度。
