## BUYER shall have the right to, by written notice to the SELLER, cancel the CONTRACT and to terminate the right of the SELLER to proceed or continue under this CONTRACT if it is found that gratuities (in the form of entertainment, gifts or otherwise), were offered or given by the SELLER, or any agent or representative of the SELLER to any officer or employee of the BUYER with a view toward securing this CONTRACT, or the making of any determinations with respect to the performing of this CONTRACT. In the event this CONTRACT is terminated as provided in this provision, BUYER shall be entitled to pursue the same remedies against the SELLER as it can pursue in the event of a material breach of the CONTRACT by the SELLER. The rights and remedies of BUYER provided in this Article or any other Article shall not be exclusive and are in addition to any other rights and remedies provided by law or under this CONTRACT.

如果买方发现卖方、或卖方的任何代理机构或代表，为了得到此合同或者为了在履行此合同时要做出任何决定，向买方的任一官员或雇员提供好处费（采取娱乐消费、赠送礼物或其他形式），一旦发现，买方有权以书面形式通知卖方解除合同终止其继续履行此合同的权利。如果此合同是因为违反了此规定终止的，买方有权参照卖方根本违约而对卖方要求相同的赔偿。在本条款或任何其他条款中规定的买方所能享有的权利和能采取的索偿行动不是单独使用的，它们可以和法律或此采购单中规定的任何其他权利和赔偿措施共同使用。
