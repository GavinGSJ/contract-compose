## During the execution of the CONTRACT, performance test / SAT or the warranty period, upon receipt of BUYER’s claim(s) in relation to the quality of CONTRACT GOODS, non-conformance, liquidated damages or violation to the provisions of this CONTRACT, SELLER shall reply to BUYER's claim not later than seven (7) days after receipt of the claim, failing which the claims will be regarded as having been accepted by SELLER and SELLER shall be deemed to waive any right to dispute such claims.

在合同执行期间、性能试验/现场验收试验或保修期内，卖方在收到买方有关合同货物质量、不合格、违约赔偿金或违反本合同规定的索赔通知后不迟于7天内应回复买方的索赔。如果卖方没有在上述时限内回复，默认卖方已接受买方的索赔要求。
