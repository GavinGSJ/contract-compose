### The insurances required to be obtained and maintained by SELLER pursuant to this Article are as follows: 根据本条规定，卖方需要购买和维持的保险如下：

- Workmen's compensation Insurance in relation to SELLER’s PERSONNEL to the minimum amount required by APPLICABLE LAW, including the laws of the state of origin of the SELLER’s expatriate employees or their equivalents; 适用法律（包括卖方外籍雇员或其同等人员的原籍国法律）要求的与卖方人员相关的最低金额的工人赔偿保险；

- Employers Liability Insurance covering injury to or sickness, disease or death of the PERSONNEL of SELLER engaged in the SUPPLY to the minimum amount required by APPLICABLE LAW, including extended cover (where applicable) for working offshore with the same limit, but in any event in an amount not less than ten million US Dollars (US$10,000,000) each and every occurrence, inclusive of costs and expenses; 雇主责任保险，承保卖方参与供货的人员的受伤或疾病、疾病或死亡，保险金额为适用法律要求的最低金额，包括离岸工作的延长保险（如适用），保险限额相同，但每次事故的保险金额不低于1000万美元（US$10,000,000），包括成本和费用；

- General Third Party Liability Insurance covering the operations of SELLER in the performance of the CONTRACT, including liability for products and post-works, cross liability and accidental pollution and contamination in an amount not less than US$15,000,000 each and every occurrence (annual aggregate in respect of products and post-works, and accidental pollution and contamination), inclusive of costs and expenses, including extended cover (where applicable) for working offshore with the same limit. Such insurance shall include BUYER Group as additional insured to the extent of the liabilities assumed by the SELLER under the CONTRACT; and一般第三方责任险，涵盖卖方在履行合同过程中的运营，包括产品和后期工作、交叉责任和意外污染和污染的责任，每次发生的金额不低于15,000,000美元（产品和后期供货以及意外污染和污染的年度总额），包括成本和费用，包括相同限额的离岸作业的延长保险（如适用）。此类保险应包括买方集团作为卖方根据合同承担的责任范围内的附加被保险人；和

- If the performance of the CONTRACT requires the use of any motor vehicles, third party and Passenger Liability insurance and other motor insurance in the amount not less than Five Million United States Dollars (US$5,000,000.00) or such amount as is required by APPLICABLE LAW, whichever is the greater, for any one occurrence; and如果履行合同需要使用任何机动车辆、第三方和乘客责任保险以及其他机动保险，金额不低于五百万美元（US$5,000,000.00）或适用法律要求的金额，以较大者为准；和

- Such other insurances as may be required by APPLICABLE LAW. 适用法律可能要求的其他保险。
