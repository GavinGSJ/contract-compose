## FORCE MAJEURE means an exceptional event or circumstance which is unforeseeable, insurmountable, outside the control of the PARTY which invokes it, not substantially attributable to either PARTY, and which renders the invoking PARTY unable to comply totally or partially with its/his obligations under this CONTRACT.

不可抗力：是指不可预见的、无法克服的、超出援引方控制范围的、实质上不可归责于任何一方的特殊事件或情况，导致援引方无法完全或部分履行其在本合同项下的义务。

Provided the foregoing criteria are fully satisfied, FORCE MAJEURE includes:

如果完全满足上述标准，则不可抗力包括：

- Natural catastrophes such as epidemic, fires, tidal wave, tsunami, lightning, earthquake, hurricane, typhoon, cyclone, volcanic activity,

  流行病、火灾、潮汐、海啸、闪电、地震、飓风、台风、气旋、火山活动等自然灾害，

- Hostilities or acts of war (whether declared or not), acts of terrorism, civil or military disturbances,

  敌对行动或战争行为（无论是否宣布）、恐怖主义行为、内乱或军事动乱，

- sabotage, riots (other than among employees of BUYER, SELLER or subcontractors), national or regional labor strikes (excluding those referred to in Article {{ref:FM-03}} i) below) and acts of any government or public authority or any representative thereof whether or not legally valid.

  蓄意破坏、暴乱（买方、卖方或分包商的员工除外）、全国或地区劳工罢工（第{{ref:FM-03}}条i）项所述情形除外）以及任何政府或公共当局或其任何代表的行为，无论是否合法。

- ionizing radiations or contamination by radioactivity from any nuclear fuel or from any nuclear wastes from the combustion of nuclear fuel or radioactive, toxic, explosive or other hazardous properties of any explosive nuclear assembly or nuclear component thereof,

  任何核燃料或核燃料燃烧产生的任何核废料的电离辐射或放射性污染，或任何爆炸性核组件或其核部件的放射性、毒性、爆炸性或其他危险特性，
