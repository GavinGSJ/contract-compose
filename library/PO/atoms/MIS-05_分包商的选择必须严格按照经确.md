## The selection of SUB-SUPPLIER shall be in compliance with the approved list, if made available in the CONTRACT. Any deviation from the list shall be approved by BUYER in writing prior to placing a SUBCONTRACT. Otherwise, the BUYER has right to reject the CONTRACT GOODS.

分包商的选择必须严格按照经确认的分包商名单（如果已在合同中提供），任何变动需在下分订单之前得到买方的书面确认。否则买方有权拒收合同货物。
