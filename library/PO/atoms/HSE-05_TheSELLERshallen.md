## The SELLER shall ensure that if any SELLER GROUP’s PERSONNEL observes or becomes aware of any practice that is not in compliance with any applicable HSSE requirement, such PERSONNEL shall immediately (a) direct that such practices be corrected to comply with this Article, and (b) notify the BUYER’s Representative of such incident(s). Upon becoming aware of such incident, SELLER’s Representative must immediately notify BUYER and perform, without delay, any corrective steps required to comply with applicable HSSE requirements. 卖方应确保，如果任何卖方集团人员观察到或意识到任何不符合任何适用HSSE要求的行为，该等人员应立即（a）指示纠正此类行为以符合本条规定，并（b）将此类事件通知买方代表。一旦意识到此类事件，卖方代表必须立即通知买方，并立即采取纠正措施，以符合适用的HSSE要求。
