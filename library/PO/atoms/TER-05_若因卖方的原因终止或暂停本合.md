## If the BUYER terminates or suspends the execution of this CONTRACT for reasons attributable to the SELLER, the SELLER shall be liable for all direct and indirect losses arising therefrom from the BUYER, including, but not limited to, the available benefits and third-party claims, the difference in the price of the re-contract, the resultant rush costs and the fines that caused the delay in the project.

若因卖方的原因终止或暂停本合同执行的，卖方应承担买方由此产生的所有直接和间接损失费用，包括但不仅限于可得利益及第三方索赔、重新签订合同的差价、由此产生的赶工费和导致项目延期的罚款等。
