## For GOODS delivery to SITE that is under BUYER’s responsibility, SELLER shall advise BUYER by email of the shipment providing shipping advice that includes CONTRACT No., name of GOODS (strictly follow the CONTRACT), quantity, value, number of packages, estimated gross weight, measurement (length, height, width), at least sixty (60) days before the date of delivery of any GOODS stipulated in the CONTRACT. The date of readiness at the port of shipment shall be confirmed by the SELLER not later than 20 days prior to the dispatch of GOODS.

对于由买方负责向现场交付的货物，卖方应在合同规定的任何货物交货日前60天，通过电子邮件向买方发出装运通知，包括合同号、货物名称（严格按照合同）、数量、价值、箱数、估计的毛重和尺寸（长、宽、高）。卖方应至少在发货前20天确认货物在港口备妥的日期。
