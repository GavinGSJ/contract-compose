## SELLER waives any right of recourse against, and shall be liable for and shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless BUYER GROUP from and against any and all INDEMNIFIED COSTS in respect of or arising out of: 卖方放弃对买方集团的任何追索权，并应对买方集团因以下原因产生的任何和所有赔偿费用承担责任，并对其进行辩护、赔偿和保护：

- any payment demanded by any authority, which payment is not otherwise expressly reimbursable under this CONTRACT, and which is in satisfaction of any claim, demand, loss, cause of action, liability or expense of SELLER; 任何当局主管部门要求的任何付款，并且合同项下未明确是可偿还的，且可作为卖方任何索赔、要求、损失、诉讼原因、责任或费用的赔偿；

- any claim brought against any member of BUYER GROUP by any member of SELLER GROUP (other than SELLER) in relation to the CONTRACT; and/or 卖方集团任何成员（卖方除外）就合同向买方集团任何成员提出的任何索赔；和/或

- debts or claims due from SELLER GROUP. 卖方集团应付的债务或索赔。
