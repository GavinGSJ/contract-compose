## The numbers of SELLER’s technical PERSONNEL required for the activities contemplated by this Article shall be discussed and fixed by both PARTIES. However, SELLER agrees, notwithstanding the foregoing, that it shall provide at all times sufficient, competent, qualified PERSONNEL to perform the SUPPLY in compliance with the provisions of the CONTRACT.

本条规定中提及的卖方需要派遣技术人员的数量应由买卖双方讨论确定。然而，尽管有上述规定，卖方同意其应始终提供足够、称职、合格的人员，以按照合同规定执行。

NOTE: The acceptance of the CONTRACT GOODS as stipulated in TECHNICAL AGREEMENT shall not relieve SELLER from his responsibility for the CONTRACT GOODS during the stipulated warranty period.

说明：按照技术协议规定的合同货物验收不免除卖方在质保期内应承担的责任。
