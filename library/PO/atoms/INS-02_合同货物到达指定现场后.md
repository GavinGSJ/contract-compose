## After the arrival of the CONTRACT GOODS at SITE/PROJECT LOCATION, BUYER shall organize the open-package inspection for a preliminary inspection on quality, specification, quantity, etc. within two (2) weeks. SELLER shall send his representative at his own expense to join in the inspection. BUYER shall inform SELLER of the date of inspection at least one (1) week ahead, and the “Open Package Inspection Report” shall be signed by the PARTIES’ representatives. However, BUYER is entitled to do the inspection by himself if SELLER failed to present at the planned inspection after notification has been given.

合同货物到达指定现场后，买方应在两周内组织对货物的质量、规格、数量等进行开箱检验工作，卖方应该自费派遣其检测员参加。买方应提前1周通知卖方开箱的日期，而后买卖双方应签署开箱检验报告。然而，如果卖方在收到通知后未能按计划参加检验，买方有权单方自己检验。
