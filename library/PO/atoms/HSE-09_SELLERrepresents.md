## SELLER represents and warrants to have given and implemented instructions to its directors, officers, employees, consultants, Subcontractors and/or agents that such persons must comply with the requirements of this Article. SELLER shall ensure that each of its Subcontractors accepts in writing, copied to BUYER (if requested), that: (i) the obligations stated in this Article apply to them, mutatis mutandis, and (ii) that the non-fulfilment by a Subcontractor of those regulations will be considered a material breach and grounds for termination in whole or in part under the Subcontract, (iii) Subcontractors must be able to evidence to BUYER the existence and enforcement of their own detailed health, safety and environmental management plan, which complies with the requirements of this Article and (iv) SELLER (and, at BUYER’s discretion, BUYER) may perform any inspections on the Subcontractor and its activities during work hours, as it, acting reasonably, sees fit. 卖方声明并保证已向其董事、高级职员、员工、顾问、分包商和/或代理人发出并执行指示，这些人员必须遵守本条的要求。卖方应确保其各分包商以书面形式接受（如有要求），并抄送给买方：（i）本条规定的义务适用于其（经必要修改后），以及（ii）分包商未履行这些规定将被视为严重违约，并有理由根据分包合同全部或部分终止合同，（iii）分包商必须能够向买方证明其自身的详细健康、安全和环境管理计划的存在和实施，该计划符合本条的要求；（iv）卖方（以及买方自行决定的买方）可以在其合理认为合适的情况下，在工作时间内对分包商及其活动进行任何检查。
