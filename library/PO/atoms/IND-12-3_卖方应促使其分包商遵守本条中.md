### SELLER shall cause its SUBCONTRACTOR to comply with the insurance requirements in this Article and shall demonstrate compliance upon BUYER’s reasonable request.

卖方应促使其分包商遵守本条中的保险要求，并应根据买方的合理要求证明其符合要求。
