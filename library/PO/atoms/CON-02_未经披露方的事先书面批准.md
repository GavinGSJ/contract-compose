## Without the Disclosing Party’s prior written approval, the Receiving Party shall not, directly or indirectly, use the Confidential Information in any other project or disclose or reveal to any third party or make public the Confidential Information and/or any part thereof, in any forms or by any means; shall not identify the Disclosing Party or its any Affiliate or any other owner of Confidential Information in any advertising, sales material, press release, public disclosure or publicity. The Receiving Party shall not be permitted to provide any third party with any advice with respect to any issue, as based on any Confidential Information provided by the Disclosing Party

未经披露方的事先书面批准，接受方不得直接或间接以任何形式或任何方式把保密信息和/或其中的任何部分，用于其他项目或披露、透露给任何第三方或者公开；不得在任何广告、销售资料、媒体、公开宣传中宣称披露方或其任何关联方或保密信息所有者为其合作方。接受方亦不得依据披露方提供的任何保密信息，就任何问题，向任何第三方作出任何建议。
