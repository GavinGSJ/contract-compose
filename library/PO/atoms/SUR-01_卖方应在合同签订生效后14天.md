## SELLER shall submit the progress schedule and ITP (Inspection and Test Plan) to BUYER for approval, within 14 days after the CONTRACT becomes effective. SELLER shall be responsible for the performance of all activities affecting quality and schedule including those of its SUBCONTRACTOR. BUYER reserves the right to review SELLER's Quality Assurance and Quality Control Procedures.

卖方应在合同签订生效后14天内提供生产进度计划及ITP计划(检验及测试计划)供买方审核。卖方对所有会影响到质量和进度的活动应承担责任，包括其分包商的此类活动。买方有权审查卖方的质量保证体系和质量控制程序。
