## Late delivery of CONTRACT GOODS 合同货物迟交

SELLER shall promptly notify BUYER of any actual or anticipated delay in delivery by email or in the monthly progress report and take all reasonable steps to avoid or mitigate such delay without additional cost to BUYER.

卖方必须将任何实际发生或预期发生的迟延及时以邮件形式或在月度进度报告中通知买方，并且应采取合理的措施来避免或减轻该迟延，且不能要求买方支付任何额外的费用。

In the event SELLER for any reason other than Force Majeure or BUYER’s default, fails to make delivery of all or any part of the CONTRACT GOODS on time as stipulated in this CONTRACT, SELLER shall pay the liquidated damages to BUYER. The liquidated damages shall be charged at the rate of {{迟交违约金_前四周}}% of the value of the delayed CONTRACT GOODS for every seven (7) days during the first four (4) weeks of such delay, and thereafter at a rate of {{迟交违约金_此后}}% for every seven (7) days of such delay. However, such liquidated damages for late delivery of the CONTRACT GOODS shall not exceed {{英文数字:迟交违约金_上限}} percent ({{迟交违约金_上限}}%) of the TOTAL CONTRACT PRICE. Any fractional days less than seven (7) days shall be deemed to be seven (7) days for the calculation of liquidated damages. In case the SELLER fails to make delivery ten weeks later than the time of shipment stipulated in the CONTRACT, the BUYER shall have the right to cancel the CONTRACT and the SELLER, in addition to any other remedies available to BUYER and regardless of such cancellation, shall immediately pay the aforesaid damages to the BUYER along with the amount already paid by BUYER to the SELLER, if any, without delay.

如果由于不可抗力或买方违约以外的任何原因，卖方没有按照合同的规定按时交付全部或部分合同货物，卖方应向买方支付违约赔偿。在此类延误的前四（4）周内，每七（7）天应按延误合同货物价值的百分之{{中文数字:迟交违约金_前四周}}（{{迟交违约金_前四周}}%）收取违约赔偿金，此后每七（7）天按百分之{{中文数字:迟交违约金_此后}}（{{迟交违约金_此后}}%）收取违约金。但是，延迟交付合同货物的违约赔偿金不得超过合同总价的百分之{{中文数字:迟交违约金_上限}}（{{迟交违约金_上限}}%）。在计算违约赔偿金时，任何不足七（7）天的天数应视为七（7）天。如果卖方未能在合同规定的装运时间后十周交货，买方有权取消合同；而卖方，除买方可利用的其他补救措施外，无论是否取消合同，均应立即向买方支付上述违约金以及买方已向卖方支付的金额（如有），不可延误。
