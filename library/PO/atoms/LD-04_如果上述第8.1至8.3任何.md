## Once any of the maximum liquidated damages amount under Article {{ref:LD-01}} to {{ref:LD-03}} is reached, the BUYER shall have the right to cancel the CONTRACT without prejudice to its other rights and remedies including the right to recover any unpaid liquidated damages.

如果上述第{{ref:LD-01}}至{{ref:LD-03}}任何一项违约金金额达到上限，买方有权在不损害其其他权利和补救措施（包括追索任何卖方未支付的违约赔偿金的权利）的情况下取消合同。
