## The SELLER shall perform the SUPPLY in accordance with APPLICABLE LAW and any HSSE requirements reasonably imposed by BUYER to give effect to OWNER’S HSSE requirements in relation to the SUPPLY. 卖方应根据适用法律、石油行业优行国际惯例和买方合理HSSE要求执行相关供货活动，以达到业主与供货相关的HSSE要求。
