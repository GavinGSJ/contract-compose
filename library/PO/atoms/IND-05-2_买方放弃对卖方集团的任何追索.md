### BUYER waives any right of recourse against, and shall be liable for and shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless SELLER GROUP from and against any and all INDEMNIFIED COSTS in respect of or arising out of injury, illness or death of a third party and/or loss or damage to the property, owned, hired or leased of a third party in each case to the extent caused by the negligence or breach of duty (whether statutory or otherwise) of any member of BUYER GROUP.

买方放弃对卖方集团的任何追索权，并应对卖方集团因第三方受伤、生病或死亡和/或因买方集团任何成员的疏忽或失职（无论是法定的或其他）导致的第三方使用、租用或租赁时产生的财产损失，以上任何和所有赔偿费用负责。
