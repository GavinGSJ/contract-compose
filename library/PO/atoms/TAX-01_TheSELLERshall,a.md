## The SELLER shall, and shall procure that all Subcontractors shall, comply with all APPLICABLE LAW concerning all company or branch office TAXATION, including administrative and registration requirements, maintaining proper accounting records and properly filing all documents required by APPLICABLE LAW to be filed. 卖方应且应确保所有分包商遵守所有公司或分支机构税收相关的所有适用法律，包括行政和注册要求，保持适当的会计记录，并妥善归档适用法律要求归档的所有文件。
