# AUDIT 审计

SELLER shall maintain complete records pertinent to all aspects of SUPPLY in accordance with this CONTRACT, accepted practice and APPLICABLE LAW. SELLER shall retain records during the SUPPLY and for a period of ten (10) years after completion of the SUPPLY or such longer period as may be required by APPLICABLE LAW. During such time BUYER shall have the right of access at all reasonable times to the equipment, material, personnel, and records of the SELLER to audit any of its activities pertaining to the SUPPLY and their health, safety, environmental and quality systems. Any findings from such audits shall be followed-up for closure as agreed between BUYER and SELLER.

卖方应根据本合同、公认惯例和适用法律保存与供货各方面相关的完整记录。卖方应在供货期间以及供货完成后十（10）年或适用法律要求的更长期限内保留记录。在此期间，买方有权在任何合理时间查阅卖方的设备、材料、人员和记录，以审计其与供货及其健康、安全、环境和质量体系相关的任何活动。此类审计的任何结果都应按照买方和卖方之间的协议进行跟踪关闭。

SELLER shall obtain equivalent rights of audit to those specified above from all Subcontractors and will cause such rights to extend to BUYER. As applicable, BUYER may have access at all reasonable times to the relevant SUPPLY site(s) for the purpose of monitoring, inspecting, witness testing the SUPPLY or any part thereof, its execution, documentation, progress and completion.

卖方应从所有分包商处获得与上述规定同等的审计权，并将此类权利延伸至买方。如适用，买方可在任何合理时间进入相关供货现场，以监督、检查、见证供货或其任何部分、其执行、文件、进度和竣工。
