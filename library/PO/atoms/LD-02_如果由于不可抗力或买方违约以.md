## Late delivery of TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION 证书及技术文件迟交

If for any reasons other than Force Majeure or BUYER’s default, TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION including certificates, have not been delivered by SELLER to BUYER in accordance with document submission procedure and the schedule given in TECHNICAL AGREEMENT of this CONTRACT, SELLER shall pay liquidated damages to the BUYER at the rate of {{迟交违约金_前四周}}% of TOTAL CONTRACT PRICE for every seven (7) days of such delay during the first four (4) weeks of such delay, and thereafter at a rate of {{迟交违约金_此后}}% of the TOTAL CONTRACT PRICE for every seven (7) days of such delay. The same liquidated damages shall be applied for the delivery of final TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION. Any fractional days less than seven (7) days shall be deemed to be seven (7) days for the calculation of the liquidated damages. The liquidated damages for late submission of TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION shall not exceed {{文件迟交违约金_上限}}% of the TOTAL CONTRACT PRICE.

如果由于不可抗力或买方违约以外的任何原因，卖方未按照文件提交程序和合同技术协议中规定的交付时间表交付技术文件和证书，卖方应在此类延误的前四（4）周内，每延误七（7）天，向买方支付总额的百分之{{中文数字:迟交违约金_前四周}}（{{迟交违约金_前四周}}%）的违约赔偿金，此后每延误七天，应向买方支付总价的百分之{{中文数字:迟交违约金_此后}}（{{迟交违约金_此后}}%）的违约金。同样的违约金应适用于最终技术资料汇编的交付。在计算违约金时，任何不足七（7）天的天数应视为七（7）天。迟交技术资料的违约赔偿金不得超过总价的百分之{{中文数字:文件迟交违约金_上限}}（{{文件迟交违约金_上限}}%）。
