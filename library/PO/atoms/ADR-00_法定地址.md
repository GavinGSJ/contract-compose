# LEGAL ADDRESSES 法定地址

All reports, notices, and other communications to be sent pursuant to CONTRACT shall be made in writing and delivered by hand or international courier, postage prepaid, and addressed to the following legal address of each party or to such changed address as shall be given by written notice or email. Notices may also be made by electronic mail.

所有依照合同发出的报告、通知和其他信函都应以书面形式，并且由专人递送或通过国际快递，邮资已付，按照下列合法地址进行传达，对于地址变更，变更方应以书面或电子邮件的形式通知对方。
