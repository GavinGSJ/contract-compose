## The loose accessories in packages or bundles shall be labeled by SELLER, indicating Contract No., name of equipment attached, name of accessories, their position No. and accessory No. in assembly drawings. Spare parts and tools shall be marked with the words “Spare Parts” or “Tools” beside the above particulars and will be shipped along with the main equipment.

卖方应对成包或成捆的零散附件贴上标签，注明合同号、随附的设备名称、附件名称、位号、在安装图上的附件号。零部件和工具除上述说明外，还应注明“零部件”或“工具”字样，并且和主要设备一起运输。
