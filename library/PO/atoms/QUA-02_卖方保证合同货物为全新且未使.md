## SELLER warrants and guarantees that the CONTRACT GOODS are brand-new and unused, made out of the best materials with first class workmanship, and comply in all respects with the quality, specifications and performance requirements as stipulated in any drawings, attachments and TECHNICAL AGREEMENT to the CONTRACT. SELLER further warrants and guarantees that the CONTRACT GOODS, when installed in accordance with the TECHNICAL AGREEMENT and the instruction of SELLER’s technical PERSONNEL and properly operated and maintained, shall be free from defect of any kind including in relation to design, material or manufacture and, fulfill satisfactorily its performance as specified in TECHNICAL AGREEMENT.

卖方保证合同货物为全新且未使用过，要使用最好的原材料和顶级工艺进行生产且完全符合合同图纸、附件、技术协议中对质量、规格和性能的要求。卖方应进一步保证当合同货物根据技术协议以及在卖方技术人员的指导下进行正确的安装、操作和保养时，货物应没有设计、原料或制造的缺陷，并且在合同质保期内满足技术协议中规定的性能要求。
