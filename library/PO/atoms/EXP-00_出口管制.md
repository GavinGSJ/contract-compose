# EXPORT CONTROLS 出口管制

In the event that the GOODS or any portion thereof are being exported from the United States, EU states, the United Kingdom or any other countries with export control, then SELLER shall be responsible for determining all export licensing requirements and obtaining all necessary export licenses and authorizations as required by the laws of the respective country, including, but not limited to, the United States Department of Defense, the United States Department of Commerce, EU’s UCC program, the Export Control Joint Unit of the United Kingdom and any other department or agency thereof that imposes any obligations relating to the export of goods, materials or intellectual property, including those of foreign governments (i.e, non-United States and non-EU countries). In such circumstances, SELLER shall also obtain a United States, EU countries’, the United Kingdom’s or any other country’s, as applicable, agent or freight forwarder to facilitate the export of any goods, and SELLER shall be solely and exclusively responsible for providing written authorization or power of attorney to its agent or freight forwarder so that SELLER's agent or freight forwarder can act on SELLER's behalf. Copies of the written authorization or power of attorney given by SELLER to SELLER's agent or freight forwarder must be delivered to BUYER as a condition to the transfer of any goods by SELLER to BUYER or SELLER's agent or freight forwarder. SELLER's agent or freight forwarder shall be listed on all export documents as the Principal Party (SELLER and BUYER) in Interest and/or Exporter, and SELLER and SELLER's agent or freight forwarder shall assume all responsibility for the proper handling, classification, and exporting of goods sold by SELLER to BUYER. Upon written request from BUYER, SELLER’s agent or freight forwarder, SELLER shall provide all necessary and sufficient technical information to assist BUYER, SELLER's agent or freight forwarder in determining the export classification status of the goods.

如果货物或其中的任何部分从美国、欧盟国家、英国或任何存在出口管制的国家出口，则卖方应负责确定所有出口许可要求，并根据相关国家法律的要求获得所有必要的出口许可和授权，包括但不限于美国国防部、美国商务部、欧盟海关法 （UCC）、英国出口管制机构（ECO），以及规定了与货物、材料或知识产权出口有关的义务的任何其他部门或机构，包括外国政府（即非美国和非欧盟国家）的部门或机构。在此等情况下，卖方亦应取得美国、欧盟国家、英国或相关国家的代理或货运代理，以促进任何货物的出口，而卖方应全权负责向其代理或货运代理提供书面授权或委托书，以便卖方的代理或货运代理可代表卖方行事。作为卖方向买方或卖方的代理人或货运代理转让任何货物的条件，卖方向卖方的代理人或货运代理提供的书面授权或委托书的副本必须交付给买方。卖方的代理人或货运代理应在所有出口文件上被列为主要利益方（卖方和买方）和/或出口商，卖方和卖方的代理人或货运代理应承担卖方出售给买方的货物的适当处理、分类和出口的所有责任。在买方、卖方的代理人或货运代理提出书面要求时，卖方应提供所有必要和充分的技术信息，以协助买方、卖方的代理人或货运代理确定货物的出口分类状态。

Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained elsewhere herein, SELLER shall be responsible for filing of and obtaining all such export licenses and authorizations for all goods, materials and intellectual property relating to the sale of the goods by SELLER to BUYER GROUP and SELLER shall release, defend, protect, indemnify and hold harmless BUYER GROUP and all of their respective officers, directors, managers, members, employees, agents, contractors, and subcontractors of all tiers from and against any loss, cost, claim, liability, suit, judgment, award or damage (including reasonable attorney's fees) on account of failing to file for and properly obtain all such export licenses and authorizations for all goods, materials, and intellectual property relating to the sale of the GOODS by SELLER to BUYER.

尽管本合同其他条款有任何相反规定，卖方应负责就卖方向买方集团出售货物所涉及的所有货物、材料和知识产权申请并取得所有出口许可证和授权；如因未申请或未适当取得上述出口许可证和授权而导致任何损失、费用、索赔、责任、诉讼、判决、裁决或损害（包括合理的律师费），卖方应免除、抗辩、保护和赔偿买方集团及其各自的管理人员、董事、经理、成员、员工、代理人以及各级承包商和分包商，并使其免受损害。
