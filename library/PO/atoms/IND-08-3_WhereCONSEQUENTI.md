### Where CONSEQUENTIAL LOSS shall mean: (i) any indirect, special, incidental, contingent or consequential damages; and (ii) in each case, whether direct or indirect, any loss of profit or anticipated profit, loss of revenue or anticipated revenue, loss of investment or opportunity, loss of product or business interruption, howsoever caused, and even in the event of the fault, negligence or strict liability of a Party or any person. 后果性损失是指:(i)任何间接的、特殊的、偶然的、或有的或后果性的损害;以及(ii)在每种情况下，无论直接或间接，任何利润或预期利润的损失、收入或预期收入的损失、投资或机会的损失、产品的损失或业务中断，无论其原因如何造成，甚至在一方或任何人的过错、疏忽或严格责任的情况下。
