## If SELLER’s performance on this CONTRACT includes experimental design, or new development SUPPLY, and such SUPPLY is paid for in whole or in part by BUYER, SELLER shall a) Promptly disclose and assign to BUYER, all technical data resulting from such SUPPLY, including all Confidential designs, processes, trade secrets, whether or not patentable, resulting from such SUPPLY, called “New Technology”, b) Maintain the “New technology” in strict confidence as using the same care as SELLER uses to protect its own proprietary information, and c) Not to disclose the “New Technology” to any Third party Entity.

如果卖方履行本合同包括实验性设计或新开发供货，且该等供货全部或部分由买方支付，则卖方应 a)及时向买方披露并转让由该供货产生的所有技术数据，包括由该供货产生的所有保密设计、工艺、商业秘密(无论是否可获得专利)，称为“新技术”; b)严格保密“新技术”，如同卖方保护其专有信息一样; c)不得向任何第三方实体披露“新技术”。
