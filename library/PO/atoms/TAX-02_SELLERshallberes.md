## SELLER shall be responsible for and shall pay or shall procure that any relevant Subcontractor is responsible for and shall pay, and shall indemnify, defend and hold the BUYER Group harmless from and against, all TAXATION assessable, assessed or imposed on the SELLER or its Subcontractors, in connection with the CONTRACT, and any claims in connection with such TAX obligations. 卖方应负责并应支付或促使任何相关分包商负责并应赔偿、保护买方集团，使其免受卖方或其分包商因本合同而应征收、评估或征收的所有税款以及与该等税款义务相关的任何索赔。
