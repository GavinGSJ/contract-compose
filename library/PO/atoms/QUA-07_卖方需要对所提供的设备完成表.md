## Painting requirements 涂装要求

- The SELLER is required to complete the surface treatment, primer construction, top coating and other related painting work on the equipment supplied. Specific surface treatment requirements, paint colors, paint coating film thickness and other requirements shall be based on the Technical Agreement signed by both parties and shall meet all the requirements stipulated in the agreement.

  卖方需要对所提供的设备完成表面处理、底漆施工、面漆涂装等相关油漆工作。具体的表面处理要求、油漆颜色、油漆涂装膜厚等要求需要依据双方签订的技术协议进行并符合协议中规定的所有要求。

- Paint brand specified by the BUYER or using the same quality brand, the SELLER needs to report to the BUYER in advance to confirm the use of other brands and to ensure that the paint used is environmentally friendly and in line with the project's classification society and industry standards.

  油漆品牌由买方指定的品牌或使用同等品质的品牌，卖方使用其他品牌时需提前报买方确认并确保所使用的油漆环保且符合本项目船级社和行业标准。

- The SELLER is required to ensure that the surface of the painted equipment is uniform, smooth and free from defects such as run-off and bubbles.

  卖方需要保证涂装后的设备表面均匀、光滑、无流挂、气泡等缺陷。

- The SELLER shall ensure that all painting processes comply with the relevant standards and specifications of the classification society and paint manufacturer for this project.

  卖方需保证所有涂装工序均符合本项目船级社和油漆厂家的相关标准和规范。

- The SELLER is responsible for the painting quality, and the warranty period must meet the requirements of the Technical Agreement. If quality issues arise during the warranty period, the SELLER shall repair or repaint free of charge.

  卖方对涂装质量负责，质保期需要满足技术协议要求。在质保期内出现质量问题，卖方应无偿修复或重新涂装。
