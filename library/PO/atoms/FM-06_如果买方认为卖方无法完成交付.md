## If completion of the delivery, in the opinion of BUYER, is made impossible or BUYER is no longer interested in further delivery of CONTRACT GOODS due to FORCE MAJEURE, BUYER shall have the right to terminate the CONTRACT by 14 days prior notice to the SELLER; or if FORCE MAJEURE has lasted (or in the joint opinion of the PARTIES is anticipated to last) more than (90) consecutive days and has affected the major part of the SUPPLY currently performed as evidenced by the PARTY invoking such FORCE MAJEURE event, either PARTY shall have the right to terminate the CONTRACT by 14 days prior notice to the other PARTY.

如果买方认为卖方无法完成交付，或者买方因不可抗力而不再有兴趣进一步交付合同货物，买方有权提前14天通知卖方终止本合同；或者，如果不可抗力已连续持续（或双方共同认为预计将持续）超过（90）天，并影响了当前执行的供货的主要部分，如援引该不可抗力事件的一方所证明的，任何一方均有权提前14天通知另一方终止本合同。
