## The SELLER shall issue a legal, formal and valid special VAT or commercial invoice, as the case may be, to the BUYER. The SELLER undertakes that, if at any time the BUYER or any third party finds that the invoice issued by the SELLER does not meet the requirements, the SELLER shall immediately issue a new invoice to the BUYER that meets the requirements, and pay to the BUYER a penalty equal to the total amount of the invoice that does not meet the requirements. If the BUYER is penalized by the relevant department because the invoice issued by the SELLER does not meet the requirements, the SELLER shall compensate the BUYER in full for the losses incurred as a result of the fine. If the SELLER is penalized by the relevant department for issuing an invoice that does not meet the requirements, the SELLER shall bear full responsibility, and such penalty shall not be a ground for lessening or exempting the SELLER from liability to the BUYER under this CONTRACT.

卖方应根据具体情况为买方开具合法、正式和有效的增值税专用发票或商业发票。卖方承诺，如买方或任何第三方在任何时候发现卖方开具的发票不符合要求，卖方应立即重新为买方开具符合要求的发票，并向买方支付与不符合要求的发票的总额相等的违约金。如买方因为卖方开具的发票不符合要求而受到有关部门的处罚，卖方还应全额赔偿买方因该处罚而受到的损失。如卖方因开具不符合要求的发票而受到有关部门的处罚，卖方应承担全部责任，且该处罚不能作为卖方减轻或免除在本合同项下应向买方承担责任的理由。
