## In the event of any loss and/or damage to the GOODS (for which SELLER is liable pursuant to this Article), SELLER shall, at SELLER’s expenses, if instructed by BUYER, replace, reconstruct, or repair such GOODS. In the event BUYER elects to himself or a third party to reconstruct, repair or replace the loss and/or damage to the same, SELLER shall pay to BUYER all associated costs incurred.

如果货物发生任何损失和/或损坏（卖方根据本条承担责任），如果买方指示，卖方应自费更换、重建或修理此类货物。如果买方选择由自身或第三方重建、修理或更换损失和/或损坏的货物，卖方应向买方支付所有相关费用。
