## If all the criteria stipulated in TECHNICAL AGREEMENT are fulfilled during performance test / SAT, an Acceptance Certificate of the CONTRACT GOODS shall be issued by BUYER and signed by the authorized representatives of the PARTIES within three (3) working days in four original copies, two copies will be kept by each PARTY for safekeeping. This signed “Acceptance Certificate” shall be deemed as the formal acceptance of the CONTRACT GOODS by BUYER.

如果合同技术协议规定的所有标准在性能测试/现场验收试验期间全部达标，合同货物的“接收证明”应由双方的授权代表在3个工作日内签订，正本一式四份，双方各执两份。此份文件将作为买方验收合同货物合格的正式证明。
