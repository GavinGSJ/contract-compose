## In case the CONTRACT GOODS are to be dispatched by air freight/parcel post, the SELLER shall, 30 days before the time of shipment as stipulated in the CONTRACT, inform the BUYER or E-mail. The SELLER shall also, immediately after dispatch of the CONTRACT GOODS, advice the BUYER according to the stipulations of Article "Shipping Advice" for the BUYER to arrange insurance and Customs clearance in time.

如果货物通过空运或者邮件寄送，卖方应在合同规定的发货日期前30天以邮件方式通知买方，并在发货后立即根据合同发货通知规定通知买方以便买方及时安排保险及清关。
