## The final prices on the CONTRACT is deemed to include all taxes which are not expressly imposed on BUYER by law, and SELLER shall indemnify and hold BUYER and the BUYER GROUP, harmless from any liability and expense by reason of SELLER’s failure to pay such taxes. 本合同最终价格应视为已包含法律未明确规定由买方承担的所有税费；如因卖方未缴纳该等税费而产生任何责任和费用，卖方应赔偿买方及买方集团并使其免受损害。
