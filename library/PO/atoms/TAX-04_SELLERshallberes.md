## SELLER shall be responsible for, indemnify, defend and hold the BUYER Group harmless from and against all claims arising out of or in connection with the CONTRACT in respect of the employment or worker status of SELLER Group PERSONNEL and all benefits and rights in connection with the same, including claims for wages, salary, holiday pay, unfair dismissal, notice or payment in lieu of notice and redundancy payments. 卖方应负责、赔偿、保护买方集团，使其免受因本合同引起的或与本合同有关的与卖方集团人员的雇佣或工人身份相关的所有索赔，以及与此相关的所有福利和权利，包括工资、工资、假日工资、不公平解雇、通知或代替通知的付款和裁员付款。
