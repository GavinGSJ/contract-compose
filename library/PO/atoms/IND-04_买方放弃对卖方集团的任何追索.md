## BUYER waives any right of recourse against, and shall be liable for and shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless SELLER GROUP from and against any and all INDEMNIFIED COSTS of whatever nature and howsoever caused, in respect of, or arising out of:

买方放弃对卖方集团的任何追索权，并应负责保护、赔偿卖方集团，使其免受因以下原因而产生的任何和所有赔偿成本的损害：

- injury, illness or death of any personnel of BUYER GROUP; and/or买方集团任何人员的受伤、生病或死亡；和/或

- loss of, or damage to the property, owned, hired or leased, of any member of BUYER GROUP except that which is under the care, custody, control, protection and/or preservation of any member of SELLER Group or is otherwise stated to be SELLER’s liability pursuant to Article {{ref:IND-02}}. 买方集团任何成员拥有、租用或租赁的财产的损失或损坏，但由卖方集团任何成员照管、保管、控制、保护和/或保存的财产或根据第{{ref:IND-02}}条规定由卖方负责的财产除外。
