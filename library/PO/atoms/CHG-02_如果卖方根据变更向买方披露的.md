## If the cost and/or schedule impact(s) disclosed by SELLER to BUYER pursuant of the changes could not be agreed upon by BUYER and are/is subject to further negotiation with SELLER, BUYER could issue a formal instruction to SELLER to implement the whole or part of the intended changes that SELLER must execute the changes as mentioned in the BUYER’s formal instruction, while having friendly negotiation with BUYER to reach a final agreement on the cost and/or schedule impact(s). Both PARTIES shall act in good faith to reach a mutual agreement on those impact(s).

如果卖方根据变更向买方披露的成本和/或进度影响不能得到买方的同意，并且需要与卖方进一步协商，买方可以向卖方发出正式指示，以实施全部或部分预期变更，卖方必须执行买方正式指示中提到的变更，同时与买方进行友好协商，以就成本和/或进度影响达成最终协议。双方应本着诚信原则就这些变更达成共同协议。
