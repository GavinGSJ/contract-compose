### SELLER shall obtain and maintain as a minimum the insurances of the types and in the amounts as set out in below and shall maintain such insurance in full force and effect for the duration of the CONTRACT (and shall provide evidence of the same on request). All such insurances shall be primary and placed with reputable and substantial insurers satisfactory to BUYER. SELLER shall bear all excesses, deductibles, or limitations incorporated therein. Such insurance shall contain insurers’ waivers of subrogation in favor of BUYER and OWNER consistent with (and to give effect to) any indemnities in this Article. The provisions of this Article shall in no way operate as a limitation of any liability assumed or indemnity given by the SELLER under the CONTRACT or of the SELLER's liability in tort, under contract or otherwise at law.

卖方应至少购买并维持下述类型和金额的保险，并应在合同期限内保持此类保险的充分效力（并应根据要求提供证据）。所有此类保险应为原保险（即投保人与保险公司直接签订），并经由买方同意的信誉良好的大型保险公司投保。卖方应承担保险中包含的所有超额、免赔额或限制。此类保险应包含保险公司放弃以买方和业主为受益人的代位求偿权，以符合本条规定的任何赔偿（并使之生效）。本条规定不得以任何方式限制卖方根据本合同承担的任何责任或给予的赔偿，也不得限制卖方根据合同或其他法律承担的侵权责任。
