## In case SELLER is liable for paying any loss or damages to BUYER, the payment shall be made by T/T within ten (10) days after SELLER has received the claim from BUYER. Otherwise, BUYER will have the right to deduct the amount from any payment made to SELLER, or to recover such an amount as much as possible from the performance bond, warranty bond or retention fund, whichever is possible. BUYER, without waiver or limitation of any rights or remedies of BUYER, shall be entitled from time to time to deduct the liquidated damages or back-charges from payment to SELLER. If there is any unrecoverable amount from the above mentioned means, BUYER shall have the right to seek for such compensation from SELLER in accordance with Article {{ref:DIS-00}} of this CONTRACT.

如果卖方应就损失损坏向买方支付罚款和/或赔偿金，卖方应在收到买方的索赔通知后10天内通过电汇支付，否则买方有权从付给卖方的任何款项里扣除，或尽可能从履约保函、质保函或者质保金中收回该款项（以可能项为准）。在不放弃或限制买方的任何权利或补救措施的情况下，买方有权不时从支付给卖方的款项中扣除违约赔偿金或补收费用。如果通过上述方式仍有任何无法收回的款项，买方有权按照本合同第{{ref:DIS-00}}条的约定向卖方要求赔偿。
