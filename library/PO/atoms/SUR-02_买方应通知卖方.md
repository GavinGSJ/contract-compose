## BUYER shall notify SELLER with the intention to dispatch BUYER’s inspection team and /or the third party inspection team at its own expense to perform pre-delivery inspection and/or test of the CONTRACT GOODS with respect to the quality, specifications, quantity, weight, packaging and requirements for safety and sanitation/hygiene in the manufacturer's workshop together with SELLER's inspectors. Such inspection does not relieve SELLER from its warranty obligations under this CONTRACT or its responsibility to furnish satisfactory CONTRACT GOODS in accordance with the CONTRACT. It is furthermore understood to be BUYER's privilege to waive the pre-delivery inspection at the workshop of manufacturer without prejudice to his right to accept CONTRACT GOODS at the final destination.

买方应通知卖方，有意自费安排买方检验人员或者第三方检验人员到制造商工厂和卖方的检验人员一起在其生产车间对合同货物在质量、规格、数量、重量、包装和合同规定的安全要求等方面的监造、催交、检验和/或测试工作，但上述检验见证工作不解除卖方提供合同项下的质保服务以及满足合同约定的货物质量的责任。此外，买方有权放弃到工厂车间参加交货前检验的权利，但不损害/不影响在最终目的地接受合同货物的权利。

Upon mutual agreement between BUYER and SELLER, BUYER's representative shall be granted access to all parts of the SELLER's plant(s) or SELLER's SUB-SUPPLIER’s plant(s) engaged in the manufacturing or processing of this Contract for the purposes of expediting, auditing the SUPPLY including any Quality Plan implementation and for the witnessing of any testing.

经买方与卖方共同商定，在进行催交、审计（例如任何质量计划的实施）以及见证测试和试验过程中，买方的代表必须被允许进入制造或执行此合同的卖方工厂或卖方的分包商工厂的任何区域。

During any joint-inspection, BUYER’s inspectors shall have the right to put forward their opinions in the course of the joint-inspection. SELLER shall give full consideration to these opinions and take necessary measures to remove the defects, and assure the quality and the delivery schedule.

在联合检查中，买方的检验员有权发表意见，卖方应充分考虑这些意见并采取必要措施消除缺陷，并且保证质量和交货时间。
