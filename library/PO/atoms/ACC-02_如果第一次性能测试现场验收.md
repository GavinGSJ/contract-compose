## If the first performance test / SAT has failed, SELLER shall make repair, replacement and/or necessary modifications to the CONTRACT GOODS as quickly as possible. All expenses incurred (i.e. SELLER’s PERSONNEL charges, materials, freight from the site to the SELLER’s factory and vice versa, customs duties, insurance premium, storage, loading and unloading, dismantling and re-installation charges and taxes etc.) shall be borne by the SELLER except to the extent that such failure is solely attributable to BUYER.

如果第一次性能测试/现场验收试验不达标，卖方应尽快进行修理、更换或对货物进行必要的更改。一切因此产生的所有费用（例如卖方人员的费用、原材料、从现场到卖方工厂往返的运费、关税、保险费、仓储费、重新安装费和税费等）均应由卖方承担，除非此不达标仅归咎于买方。
