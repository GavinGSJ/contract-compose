## BUYER shall be entitled to make changes in this CONTRACT and shall inform SELLER of BUYER’s intention, in a written notice. If SELLER believes that such change affects the price or delivery date of the CONTRACT GOODS, SELLER shall so notify BUYER in writing (with adequate supporting information) within five (5) working days after receipt of said written notice.

买方有权对此合同作出变更，并以书面形式将买方意向告知卖方。如果卖方认为此类变更会影响合同货物的价格或交货日期，卖方必须在收到上述书面指令后的五 (5)个工作日之内书面通知买方（并提供充分的支持文件）。
