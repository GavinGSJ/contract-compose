## SELLER shall mark the following on the four (4) adjacent sides of each package with fadeless paint in conspicuous English and the words such as Contract No., name of CONTRACT GOODS, item No. of CONTRACT GOODS, destination, package/bale No., gross & net weight （Kg）, measures (length X width X height, cm, and the words “THIS SIDE UP”, “HANDLE WITH CARE”, “KEEP DRY”, etc..), shipping mark ,and the Consignee.

卖方应用不褪色漆或颜料在每一个货物包装箱的四个相邻面用醒目的英文标明合同号、货物名称、货物序号、目的地、箱包号、毛/净重（千克）、尺寸（长X宽X高，单位：厘米）以及 “此面向上”、“小心轻放”、“保持干燥”等示语，还有运输标志和收货人的名称。
