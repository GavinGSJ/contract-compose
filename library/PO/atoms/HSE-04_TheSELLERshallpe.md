## The SELLER shall perform, or cause to be performed, at its own expense, all actions necessary to protect: (i) all persons from any exposure to, or hazard from, hazardous material and shall adopt all measures needed to reduce, as low as reasonably possible, any injury or damage to people or property; (ii) and the environment from, and to avoid damage and nuisance to persons and property, resulting from the provision of the SUPPLY, shall not, under any circumstances, cause or permit, in connection with the provision of the SUPPLY, the discharge, emission or release of any hazardous substance or material except in compliance with APPLICABLE LAW and shall immediately report any such discharges, emissions or releases to BUYER. 卖方应自费采取或促使他人采取一切必要措施，以保护：（i）所有人员免受危险材料的接触或危害，并应采取一切必要的措施，尽可能减少对人员或财产的伤害或损害；（ii）环境，为避免因供货而对人员和财产造成损害和滋扰。在任何条件下，除符合适用法律标准，不得造成或允许与供货活动相关的任何有害物质或材料的固、液排放或气体释放，并应将此类固、液排放或气体释放立即报告给买方。
