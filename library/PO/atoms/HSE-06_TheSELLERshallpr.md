## The SELLER shall provide to BUYER, on request, a monthly report of any HSSE incidents and accidents the overall number of worked hours at the workplace, the causes, reasons and circumstances of each occurred injury or accident and SELLER’s remedial measures in respect thereof. 应要求，卖方应向买方提供任何HSSE事件和事故的月度报告，包括工作场所的总工作时间、每次发生的伤害或事故的原因、原因和情况以及卖方就此采取的补救措施。
