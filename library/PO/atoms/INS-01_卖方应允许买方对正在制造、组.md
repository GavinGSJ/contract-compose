## SELLER shall allow BUYER the right for inspection of CONTRACT GOODS during fabrication, assembly and/or prior to shipment, to check the accuracy of related documentation on weights, quality, quantity and certificates, if they are conforming to CONTRACT specifications.

卖方应允许买方对正在制造、组装或装运期间和/或前的合同货物进行检查，以检查重量、质量、数量和证书等相关文件是否符合合同规格。
