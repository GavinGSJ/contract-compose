## 非经买方事先书面同意，卖方不得发布广告、公众号、公告、申明或向任何其他方（包括任何媒体等）提供关于双方的合作关系、项目内容、本合同的存在和/或条款的任何信息，不得以任何方式使用包括援引买方的名称、 logo、商标、商号、项目等信息用于任何用途。如卖方违反本条约定，买方有权要求卖方立即停止、改正违约行为并承担违约金，为合同总额的{{宣传违约金比例}}%，如该等违约金不足以弥补买方因此遭受的损失，卖方应补偿买方的全部损失。

Without the prior written consent of the BUYER, the SELLER shall not publish advertisements, official accounts, announcements, statements, or provide any information to any other party (include any media etc.) regarding the cooperation relationship between the two parties, the project content, the existence and/or terms of this CONTRACT, and shall not use any information in any way, including referencing the BUYER's name, logo, trademark, trade name, project, etc., for any purpose. If the SELLER violates this agreement, the BUYER has the right to demand that the SELLER immediately stop, correct the breach of contract, and bear a penalty of 5% of the total contract amount. If such penalty is not enough to compensate for the losses suffered by the BUYER as a result, the SELLER shall compensate the BUYER for all losses incurred.
