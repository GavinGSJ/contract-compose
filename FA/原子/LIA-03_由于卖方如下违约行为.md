## 由于卖方如下违约行为，买方有权解除合同，卖方应按合同总价的{{解约违约金比例}}%向买方支付违约金；买方要求退货的，卖方应在买方的退货要求提出后15日内返还退货部分所对应的全部款项Due to the following breach of contract by the SELLER, the BUYER has the right to terminate the contract, and the SELLER shall pay a penalty of {{解约违约金比例}}% of the total contract price to the BUYER; If the BUYER requests a return, the SELLER shall return all the corresponding payment for the returned part within 15 days after the BUYER's request for return is made：

- 卖方延迟交货超过4周的If the SELLER delays delivery by more than 4 weeks；

- 卖方不按照《技术协议》要求履约，包括但不限于使用材质不符、质量缺陷、试验项目不符、填报虚假报告、隐瞒质量问题、未按照要求提供技术服务等不诚信行为The SELLER fails to fulfill the requirements of the Technical Agreement, including but not limited to using materials that do not comply, quality defects, inspection tests that do not comply, filling out false reports, concealing quality issues, and failing to provide technical services as required, and other dishonest behaviors；

- 卖方未经买方书面同意将本合同下卖方工作转包给其它任何第三方（双方约定的外购件除外），买方有权拒收或立即解除合同，除上述违约金外，买方有权任何时间内对卖方进行处罚，处罚金额包含但不限于对买方造成的直接和间接损失Without the written consent of the BUYER, if the SELLER subcontracts the SELLER's work under this CONTRACT to any other third party (excluding the externally purchased parts agreed upon by both parties), the BUYER has the right to refuse or immediately terminate the contract. In addition to the above-mentioned liquidated damages, the BUYER has the right to impose penalties on the SELLER at any time, including but not limited to direct and indirect losses caused to the BUYER；

- 因设备性能考核（以《技术协议》为准）不合格或质保期内出现严重质量问题，卖方在买方指定的期限内无法修补更换或拒绝修补更换If the equipment performance test (based on the Technical Agreement) fails or serious quality problems occur during the warranty period, the SELLER is unable to repair or refuse to repair or replace within the period specified by the BUYER；

- 卖方无法继续履行合同或因卖方导致的合同无法继续执行The SELLER is unable to continue performing the contract or the contract cannot be continued due to the SELLER's reasons；

- 卖方违反本合同第{{ref:WAR-00}}条、第{{ref:IP-00}}条承诺的The SELLER's breach of commitments under Article {{ref:WAR-00}} and Article {{ref:IP-00}} of this CONTRACT；

- 卖方违反本合同第{{ref:CON-00}}条并给买方造成重大损失的If the SELLER violates Article {{ref:CON-00}} of this CONTRACT and causes significant losses to the BUYER;

- 其他严重违反合同且未能在买受人指定期限内纠正的情形Other serious violations of the contract that have not been corrected within the specified period by the BUYER.
