## 卖方同意并确保给予买方或业主与买方代表自由进出其分包商和/或分包商处所的权利并提供便利，以便买方或业主与买方代表对卖方的分包商/子供应商的工作进展，以及催交与检验等进行检查、协调、指导或要求。SELLER shall agrees to secure the right of free access to its sub-supplier’s and/or subcontractors’premises is granted to BUYER or the OWNER together with BUYER representatives and shall be provided convenience for Buyer's or Owner's and Buyer's Representative's inspecting, coordinating, directing, or requesting the progress of work of Seller's sub-subcontractors/sub-suppliers.
