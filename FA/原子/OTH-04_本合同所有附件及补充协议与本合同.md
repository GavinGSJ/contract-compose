## 本合同所有附件及补充协议与本合同具有同等法律效力。效力优先顺序：项目特殊条款、补充协议、本合同正文、技术协议、本合同附件、合同授予通知书、报价文件、询价文件。 All attachments and supplementary agreements to this CONTRACT shall have the same legal effect as this CONTRACT. Priority order of effectiveness from highest to the lowest shall be: PROJECT SPECIAL REQUIREMENTS, supplementary agreement, main body of this CONTRACT, Technical Agreement, annexes to this CONTRACT, letter of award, quotation document, inquiry document.
