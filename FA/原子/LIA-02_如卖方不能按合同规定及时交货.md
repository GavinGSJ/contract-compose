## 如卖方不能按合同规定及时交货，卖方应向买方支付延期交货违约金，且该违约金，买方可以从应支付给卖方的剩余合同货款中扣除。延期交货4周以内，每延期交货一周，按合同总价的{{延期违约金_前4周}}%支付迟交违约金，延期交货第5周起，每周按合同总价的{{延期违约金_第5周起}}%支付迟交违约金，不足一周按一周计算。If the SELLER fails to deliver the GOODS in a timely manner as stipulated in the contract, the SELLER shall pay the BUYER a penalty for delayed delivery, and the BUYER may deduct this penalty from the remaining contract payment due to the SELLER. Within 4 weeks of delayed delivery, a penalty of {{延期违约金_前4周}}% of the total contract price shall be paid for each week of delayed delivery. Starting from the 5th week of delayed delivery, a penalty of {{延期违约金_第5周起}}% of the total contract price shall be paid for each week of delayed delivery. If the delay is less than one week, the penalty shall be calculated as one week.
