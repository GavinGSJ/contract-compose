## 由卖方负责承运的，卖方负责装运的全部工作，并确保标的物安全地到达交货地点，货物毁损灭失的风险由卖方负责。卖方应自费投保相应的运输保险。

If the SELLER is responsible for the transportation, the SELLER is responsible for all the shipping related works and ensures that the GOODS arrives safely at the delivery location. The risk of damage or loss of the GOODS is the SELLER's responsibility. The SELLER shall purchase corresponding transportation insurance at their own expense.

在启运之前，卖方应根据买方的要求将加盖卖方公章的保险凭证复印件快递给买方，如买方有异议的，卖方须按照买方的要求重新投保。Before shipment, the SELLER shall send a copy of the insurance certificate stamped with the SELLER's official stamp to the BUYER by express delivery according to the BUYER's request. If the BUYER has any objections, the SELLER shall re insure according to the BUYER's request.
