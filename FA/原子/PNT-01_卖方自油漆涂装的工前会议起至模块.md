## 卖方自油漆涂装的工前会议起至模块离开买方交付给业主时止将根据实际情况，为买方免费提供技术服务。除非另有说明，技术服务的职责为：检测、记录、报告及按照规格书要求检验，包括但不限于记录卖方产品在买方现场进行施工的环境和其它条件的任何工作日志、质量保证或质量控制，在该施工期间和施工结束时所取得的观察和测量的事实记录。卖方服务人员需现场确认涂装过程和质量符合技术要求，并填写 [供应商现场服务确认单]，并经买方施工专业工程师、施工经理确认。

The SELLER will, from the pre-job start up meeting for application until the project delivery the BUYER and is delivered to the OWNER, according to the actual circumstances, provide technical services free of charge. Unless otherwise been specified, the role of technical service representative(s) would be test, log, report, inspect as per the Technical Specification, including the completion of any working logs, quality assurance or quality control recording the environmental and other conditions under which application of SELLER’s product are made at BUYER’s site, and the record of the factual observations and measurements noted during and at the conclusion of such application. SELLER’s technical service representative must fill out the [Supplier's on-site service confirmation form] and obtain confirmation from the BUYER's construction professional engineer and construction manager, after complete the service work.

当需要时，买方将向卖方的技术服务代表提供相关的图纸、技术资料、办公场所及其他便利。所有的图纸、计算数据、数据、测试报告、基本/详细/生产设计、软件、电子程序及文件均为买方的财产，任何人不能转发、分发或者投递给未经授权的第三方，任何的违背将被记录在案，将对违背者诉诸法律。When applicable, BUYER shall provide SELLER technical service representative(s) with the relevant drawings, technical data, site office and other conveniences. All drawings, calculation data, data, test report, Basic/Detail/production designs, software, electronic program and document would be the property of Wison New Energies Co., Ltd, anyone shouldn’t delivery or/and transfer or/and publish to the unauthorized third parties, any violate would be noted, the violator would be lawing.
