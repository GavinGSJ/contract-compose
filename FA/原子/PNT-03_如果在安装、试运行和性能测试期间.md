## 如果在安装、试运行和性能测试期间，由于卖方技术人员的错误指导造成设备损坏，设备损坏造成的所有损失应由卖方承担。

If the equipment’s damaged during erection, test-run and performance test due to the wrong instruction of SELLER’s technicians, all the losses occurred related to equipment damage shall be borne by SELLER.

卖方技术人员的技术指导应以书面的形式提交，包括但不限于产品说明书，施工程序，施工建议，培训材料，信息报告等。Technical instructions by SELLER's technical PERSONNEL shall be submitted in written form, including but not limited to product Data sheet, construction procedures, construction suggestions, training materials, information reports, etc.
