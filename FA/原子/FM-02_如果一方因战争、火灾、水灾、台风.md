## 如果一方因战争、火灾、水灾、台风、地震、政府行为不可抗力使其履行合同规定的任何义务受到阻碍，则可根据受影响的程度顺延合同履行期限，并可根据不可抗力的影响，部分或全部免予承担违约责任。但是，即使发生前述不可抗力，如果卖方仍可采取其它补救措施或方案，如选择其它运输路线等，则卖方不得以不可抗力为由免责。若一方违约在先，不得以此后发生不可抗力为由免除其违约的责任。

If one party is hindered in fulfilling any obligations under the contract due to war, fire, flood, typhoon, earthquake, or government force majeure, the contract performance period may be extended according to the degree of impact, and partial or full exemption from breach of contract liability may be granted based on the impact of force majeure. However, even if the aforementioned force majeure occurs, if the SELLER can still take other remedial measures or solutions, such as choosing other transportation routes, the SELLER shall not be exempted from liability on the grounds of force majeure. If one party breaches the contract first, it shall not be exempted from liability for breach of contract on the grounds that force majeure occurs afterwards.
