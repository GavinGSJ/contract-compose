## 合同有效期为自本合同生效之日起到权利义务履行结束之日止，无论合同解除或终止，双方的保密义务均持续有效。如经买方和卖方双方协商一致，本合同的有效期可以延长。

The validity period of the contract is from the effective date of the contract to the end of the performance of rights and obligations. Regardless of the termination or termination of the contract, the confidentiality obligations of both parties shall remain valid. If mutually agreed upon by both the BUYER and SELLER, the validity period of this CONTRACT may be extended.
