## 若需对买方或项目业主方人员进行培训，卖方负责提供培训教材，对被培训人员进行本合同标的物的操作和维修的培训。培训教材的深度应能满足被培训人员操作、维修、拆卸、重新组装、调整和修复生产设备的需要，使被培训人员能够正确、熟练地操作和维护合同标的物。培训时间安排在合同标的物试运行前进行，在此项培训结束前，不应认为合同标的物已经按照合同规定的接收要求被买方接受。因培训工作的缺失，造成被培训人员错误操作和维护合同标的物而造成的损失，由卖方承担责任。If training is required for personnel of the BUYER or project owner, the SELLER is responsible for providing training materials and providing training on the operation and maintenance of the subject matter of this CONTRACT to the trainees. The depth of the training materials should meet the needs of the trainees to operate, maintain, disassemble, reassemble, adjust, and repair production equipment, so that the trainees can correctly and proficiently operate and maintain the contract subject matter. The training schedule should be arranged before the trial operation of the contract subject matter. Before the end of this training, it should not be considered that the contract subject matter has been accepted by the BUYER in accordance with the acceptance requirements stipulated in the contract. The SELLER shall be responsible for any losses caused by the trainees' incorrect operation and maintenance of the contract subject matter due to the lack of training work.
