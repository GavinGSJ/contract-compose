## 买方付款前，卖方须为买方开具合法、正式和有效的增值税专用发票，否则买方有权拒绝付款且不视为违约。卖方承诺，如买方或任何第三方在任何时候发现卖方开具的发票不符合要求，卖方应立即重新为买方开具符合要求的发票，并向买方支付与不符合要求的发票的总额相等的违约金。如买方因为卖方开具的发票不符合要求而受到有关部门的处罚，卖方还应全额赔偿买方因该处罚而受到的损失。如卖方因开具不符合要求的发票而受到有关部门的处罚，卖方应承担全部责任，且该处罚不能作为卖方减轻或免除按照上述约定应向买方承担责任的理由。

Before the BUYER makes payment, the SELLER must issue a legal, formal, and valid VAT special invoice to the BUYER. Otherwise, the BUYER has the right to refuse payment and shall not be considered a breach of contract. The SELLER promises that if the BUYER or any third party discovers at any time that the invoice issued by the SELLER does not meet the requirements, the SELLER shall immediately issue a new invoice that meets the requirements for the BUYER and pay a penalty equal to the total amount of the non-compliant invoice to the BUYER. If the BUYER is punished by relevant authorities due to the invoice issued by the SELLER not meeting the requirements, the SELLER shall also fully compensate the BUYER for the losses incurred due to such punishment. If the SELLER is punished by relevant departments for issuing invoices that do not meet the requirements, the SELLER shall bear full responsibility, and this punishment cannot be used as a reason for the SELLER to reduce or exempt the responsibility to the BUYER according to the above agreement.
