## 交（提）货方式：按（{{交货方式}}）执行。Delivery (pick-up) method: Execute according to ({{交货方式}}).

- 卖方送货或发运至买方的施工现场或仓库，或买方指定的其它地点The SELLER shall deliver or ship the GOODS to the BUYER's construction site or warehouse, or other location designated by the BUYER。

- 买方在卖方工厂、仓库或其他指定地点提货，卖方配合装货并捆扎固定The BUYER shall pick up the GOODS at the SELLER's factory, warehouse, or other designated location, and the SELLER shall cooperate in loading and tying them in place。
