## 卖方及其委托的承运人应遵照《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法》及相关运输安全规定，运输车辆和驾驶人符合其要求，并遵守附件六《惠生清能项目HSE管理要求》。货物运输期间的安全事故责任与买方无关。

The SELLER and its authorized carrier shall comply with the Road Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and relevant transportation safety regulations, transport vehicles and drivers shall meet their requirements, and comply with Annex 6 "WNE HSE Management Requirements". The responsibility for safety accidents during the transportation of GOODS is not related to the BUYER.
