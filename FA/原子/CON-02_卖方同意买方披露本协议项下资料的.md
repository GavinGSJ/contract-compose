## 卖方同意买方披露本协议项下资料的唯一目的是履行本协议约定义务，卖方承诺不将因此而取得的对方秘密信息用于其他任何用途，任何将秘密信息用于其他用途的行为均被视为对保密义务的违反。

The SELLER agrees that the sole purpose of the BUYER's disclosure of information under this agreement is to fulfill its obligations under this agreement. The SELLER undertakes not to use the confidential information obtained from the other party for any other purpose, and any use of the confidential information for any other purpose shall be deemed a breach of confidentiality obligations.
