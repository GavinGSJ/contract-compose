## 卖方（包括运输商）在送货和调试期间必须遵循买方的HSE管理规定，佩带劳动防护用品。若卖方未遵循买方的HSE管理规定或因卖方原因发生的一切安全事故，由卖方承担全部责任。

The SELLER (including the logistic agent) must comply with the BUYER's HSE management regulations and wear personal protection equipment during delivery and commissioning. If the SELLER fails to comply with the BUYER's HSE management regulations or any safety accidents occur due to the SELLER's reasons, the SELLER shall bear full responsibility.
