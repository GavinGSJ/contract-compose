## 卖方保证所供标的物或者服务拥有相关知识产权或得到授权许可，不存在对任何第三方的专利权和技术秘密、商标权和商业秘密等知识产权的侵权或对其物权的侵权。若发生侵权情况，卖方保证负责解决并承担由此产生的全部法律责任。若因此而给买方带来经济损失的，卖方应承担赔偿责任，赔偿买方因此而遭受的经济损失，该等经济损失包括但不限于直接损失、可得利益及第三方索赔等间接损失、买方为处理该等争议或纠纷而支出的律师费用、差旅费用、调查取证费用、诉讼/仲裁费用以及其它合理费用等。The SELLER guarantees that the GOODS or service provided possesses relevant intellectual property rights or has obtained authorized licenses, and there is no infringement of any third-party intellectual property rights such as patents, technical secrets, trademarks, and trade secrets, or any infringement of their property rights. If any infringement occurs, the SELLER guarantees to be responsible for resolving and bearing all legal liabilities arising therefrom. If this causes economic losses to the BUYER, the SELLER shall bear the liability for compensation and compensate the BUYER for the economic losses suffered as a result. Such economic losses include but are not limited to direct losses, obtainable benefits, indirect losses such as third-party claims, legal expenses incurred by the BUYER in handling such disputes or disputes, travel expenses, investigation and evidence collection expenses, litigation/arbitration expenses, and other reasonable expenses.
