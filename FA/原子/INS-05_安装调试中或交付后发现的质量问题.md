## 安装调试中或交付后发现的质量问题，卖方在接到买方通知后48小时内须派出专业人员到达指定地点处理，直至买方验收合格。因此增加的人员、费用由卖方承担。If any quality issues are found during installation commissioning or after delivery, the SELLER shall dispatch professional personnel to the designated location within 48 hours of receiving the BUYER's notice to handle them until the BUYER's acceptance is qualified. Therefore, the additional personnel and expenses shall be borne by the SELLER.
