# 权利担保条款Rights Guarantee Clause

卖方承诺，其所出售的标的物不侵犯任何第三方的权利（包括但不限于所有权、知识产权等），并且任何第三人都不会就该标的物向买方或标的物最终用户或项目业主主张任何权利（包括但不限于所有权、知识产权等）。若因卖方所出售的标的物侵犯第三方的权利而引起任何争议或纠纷（包括但不限于行政机关对买方的调查、诉讼和/或仲裁等），由卖方负责解决。若因此而给买方带来经济损失的，卖方应承担赔偿责任，赔偿买方因此而遭受的经济损失，该等经济损失包括但不限于直接损失、可得利益及第三方索赔等间接损失、买方为处理该等争议或纠纷而支出的律师费用、差旅费用、调查取证费用、诉讼/仲裁费用以及其它合理费用等。

The SELLER undertakes that the GOODS sold does not infringe upon the rights of any third party (including but not limited to ownership, intellectual property, etc.), and no third party will assert any rights (including but not limited to ownership, intellectual property, etc.) against the BUYER or the end user or project owner of the GOODS. If any disputes or controversies arise due to the infringement of the rights of third parties by the GOODS sold by the SELLER (including but not limited to investigations, lawsuits, and/or arbitration by administrative authorities against the BUYER), the SELLER shall be responsible for resolving them. If this causes economic losses to the BUYER, the SELLER shall be liable for compensation and compensate the BUYER for the economic losses suffered as a result. Such economic losses include but are not limited to direct losses, obtainable benefits, and indirect losses such as third-party claims. The BUYER shall also bear legal expenses, travel expenses, investigation and evidence collection expenses, litigation/arbitration expenses, and other reasonable expenses incurred by the BUYER in handling such disputes or disputes.

如因卖方违反上述承诺导致买方或标的物最终用户或项目业主无法正常使用、处置标的物的，买方有权要求卖方采取以下任一救济措施：1）将其提供的货物更换为其他不侵犯第三方权利的货物；2）卖方向相关第三方购买或取得相应的权利；3）终止本协议，卖方退还已收到的全部合同金额并按照本合同第{{ref:CHG-06}}条约定承担相应的责任。

If the SELLER violates the above commitments, resulting in the BUYER or the end user or project owner of the GOODS being unable to use or dispose of the GOODS normally, the BUYER has the right to request the SELLER to take any of the following remedial measures: 1) replace the GOODS it provides with other GOODS that do not infringe on the rights of third parties; 2) The SELLER shall purchase or acquire corresponding rights from relevant third parties; 3) Terminate this agreement, the SELLER shall refund all contract amounts received and assume corresponding responsibilities in accordance with Article {{ref:CHG-06}} of this CONTRACT.
