## 本合同约定的工作内容完成之后，买方有权要求卖方返还前述资料。未经买方许可，卖方不得留存前述资料（包括复印件）。After the completion of the work content stipulated in this CONTRACT, the BUYER has the right to request the SELLER to return the aforementioned materials. Without the BUYER's permission, the SELLER shall not retain the aforementioned information (including copies).
