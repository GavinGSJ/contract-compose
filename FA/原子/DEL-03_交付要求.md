## 交付要求：标的物发运或送货的，卖方应提前（{{发货通知天数}}）天将[发货通知单]由惠生一体化平台推送给买方合同经办人及收货人。如规定买方派人到卖方现场验收或监造的，必须由买方检验合格，并出具[产品检验放行单]后，卖方才能组织发货，运输超限物资的，卖方应提供标的物运输定位信息给合同收货人，具体详见附件三《交货要求》。Delivery Requirements: If the GOODS is shipped or delivered, the SELLER shall submit [Delivery Notice] to the BUYER's contract agent and consignee on the Wison Integrated Platform ({{发货通知天数}}) days in advance. If it is stipulated that the BUYER shall send personnel to the SELLER's site for acceptance or supervision, the BUYER must inspect and pass the inspection and issue a Product Inspection Release Form before the SELLER can organize shipment. If the transportation of oversized materials is exceeded, the SELLER shall provide the transportation positioning information of the GOODS to the contract consignee. For details, please refer to Annex 3 "Delivery Requirements".
