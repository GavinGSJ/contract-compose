## 标的物的所有权自交付之日起转移给买方，因质量问题买方拒绝接受以及本合同另有约定的除外The ownership of the GOODS shall be transferred to the BUYER from the date of delivery, unless the BUYER refuses to accept it due to quality issues or otherwise specified in this CONTRACT.
