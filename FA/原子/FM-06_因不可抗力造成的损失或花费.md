## 因不可抗力造成的损失或花费，由合同双方自行承担。当不可抗力事由结束，受影响方应及时通知另一方并恢复合同履行。卖方不得以不可抗力为由向买方主张任何额外费用。

Any losses or expenses caused by force majeure shall be borne by both parties to the contract. When the cause of force majeure ends, the affected party shall promptly notify the other party and resume the performance of the contract. The SELLER shall not claim any additional costs from the BUYER on the grounds of force majeure.
