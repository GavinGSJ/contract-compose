## 如果因已根据第{{ref:FM-03}}款的规定发出通知的不可抗力，使合同履行受到阻碍已持续90天，或由于所通知的不可抗力断续阻碍合同执行累计超过120天，任一方可向对方发出终止合同的通知。在此情况下，合同终止应在该通知发出7天后生效。

If the performance of the contract is hindered for a continuous period of 90 days due to force majeure notified in accordance with Article {{ref:FM-03}}, or if the notified force majeure has intermittently hindered the execution of the contract for a cumulative period of more than 120 days, either party may issue a notice of termination to the other party. In this case, the termination of the contract shall take effect 7 days after the notice is issued.
