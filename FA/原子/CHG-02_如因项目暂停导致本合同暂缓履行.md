## 如因项目暂停导致本合同暂缓履行，卖方在收到暂停书面通知后，应停止执行本合同，并应对合同下的物资进行保存和保护。在合同暂停时间不超过（{{免费暂停天数}}）天的情况下，卖方不应收取任何额外费用。超过（{{免费暂停天数}}）天的情况下，由此产生的仓储或保养费等费用，双方协商确定。

If the suspension of the project results in the suspension of the performance of this CONTRACT, the SELLER shall, upon receiving a written notice of suspension, cease the execution of this CONTRACT and shall preserve and protect the materials under the contract. If the contract is suspended for no more than ({{免费暂停天数}}) days, the SELLER shall not charge any additional fees. Any storage or maintenance fees incurred beyond ({{免费暂停天数}}) days shall be negotiated and determined by both parties.
