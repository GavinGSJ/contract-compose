## 买方有权根据自身需求或业主原因变更本合同内容，并以书面形式向卖方发出变更通知，卖方必须在收到上述书面指令后的二个工作日之内书面回复买方变更对合同产生的影响（包含费用和交期），若卖方未能按时回复，视为买方变更对合同不产生影响（包含费用和交期）。如该回复不能得到买方的同意，并且需要与卖方进一步协商，买方可以先向卖方发出正式指示，以实施全部或部分预期变更，卖方必须执行买方正式指示中提到的变更。同时与买方进行友好协商，以就成本和或进度影响达成最终协议。双方应本着诚信原则就这些变更达成协议。

The BUYER has the right to modify the contents of this CONTRACT according to their own needs or the owner's reasons, and send a change notice to the SELLER in writing. The SELLER must reply in writing to the BUYER within two working days after receiving the above written instructions on the impact of the change on the contract (including costs and delivery time). If the SELLER fails to reply on time, it shall be deemed that the BUYER's change has no impact on the contract (including costs and delivery time). If the response cannot obtain the BUYER's consent and further negotiation with the SELLER is required, the BUYER may first issue formal instructions to the SELLER to implement all or part of the expected changes, and the SELLER must execute the changes mentioned in the BUYER's formal instructions. Simultaneously engage in friendly negotiations with the BUYER to reach a final agreement on cost and/or schedule impacts. Both parties should reach an agreement on these changes in good faith.

在双方达成最终协议后，将发布合同修正案，正式确定对合同的修改。该修正案应视为合同完整的组成部分，与合同正文具有同样的法律效力。无论是买方还是卖方提出的潜在变更，在商议过程中，必须确保原合同的持续执行不受影响。

After both parties reach a final agreement, a contract amendment will be issued to formally determine the modifications to the contract. This amendment should be regarded as an integral part of the contract and have the same legal effect as the main text of the contract. Whether it is a potential change proposed by the BUYER or the SELLER, during the negotiation process, it is necessary to ensure that the continuous execution of the original contract is not affected.
