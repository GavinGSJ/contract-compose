## Definitions 

AFFILIATE means any entity controlled, directly or indirectly, by a company, any entity that controls, directly or indirectly, such company, or any entity directly or indirectly under common control with such company. For this purpose, "control" of any entity or person means ownership of a majority of the voting power of the entity or person.

关联公司指由某一公司直接或间接控制的任何实体、直接或间接控制该公司的任何实体，或与该公司直接或间接同受共同控制的任何实体。就本定义而言，对任何实体或人员的"控制"指持有该实体或人员过半数的表决权。

APPLICABLE LAW means any treaty, law, decree, order, regulation, decision, statute, ordinance, rule, directive, code or other document that has legal force according to the terms of any system of law, including, without limitation, local law, the laws of any other state or part thereof or international law, and which creates or purports to create any requirement or rule that may affect, restrict, prohibit or expressly allow the terms of this contract or any activity contemplated or carried out under this contract.

适用法律是指任何条约、法律、法令、命令、条例、决定、法规、条例、规则、指令、法典或其他从属任何法律体系（包括但不限于当地法律、任何其他国家或其部分法律或国际法）的条款以及具有法律效力的文件，以致影响、限制、禁止或明确允许本合同条款或根据本合同计划或开展的任何活动。

BUYER GROUP includes the following: 买方集团包括：

- BUYER and its AFFILIATES; 买方及其附属公司；

- BUYER’s joint venture partners (if any) involved in a joint venture pertaining to the SUPPLY; 参与与供货相关的合资企业的买方合资伙伴（如有）；

- BUYER’s contractors (other than SELLER) and their subcontractors (of any tier); 买方承包商（卖方除外）及其分包商（任何层级）；

- OWNER, OWNER’s co-ventures and OWNER and its co-ventures’ AFFILIATES; and业主、业主的合营者和业主及其合营者附属公司；和

- the directors, officers, employees, consultants, advisors and agents of any entity listed in this definition; but shall not include any member of SELLER GROUP. 本定义所列任何实体的董事、高管、员工、顾问、咨询和代理人；但不包括卖方集团的任何成员。

Delivery Date means the contractual date whereupon complete delivery of the CONTRACT GOODS is due.

交货日期是指合同中规定的货物应完全交付的日期。

{{设施简称}} means the {{设施全称_en}} to be supplied by BUYER to OWNER for the purpose of the PROJECT.

{{设施简称}}是指买方为本项目向业主提供的{{设施全称}}。

GOODS, MATERIALS, OR EQUIPMENT means all equipment, materials, spares, consumables, tools, supplies, software, products, and other goods as applicable, to be provided by SELLER and sold to BUYER under the CONTRACT in accordance with the TECHNICAL AGREEMENT.

“货物”或“合同货物”是指根据技术协议，由卖方根据合同提供并出售给买方的所有设备、材料、备件、消耗品、工具、供应品、软件、产品和其他适用货物。

INDEMNIFIED COSTS or CLAIMS means any and all losses, damages, liabilities, claims, demands, actions, proceedings, payments, costs, expenses (including, with no limitation, legal costs, tribunal costs, experts’ and investigative fees), fines, penalties and interest.

赔偿费用或索赔是指任何和所有损失、损害、责任、索赔、要求、诉讼、付款、成本、费用（包括但不限于法律费用、法庭费用、专家和调查费用）、罚款、惩罚和利息。

OWNER means {{业主名称}} or its permitted successor or assignee.

业主是指{{业主名称}}或其许可的继承人或受让人。

PARTIES means both the BUYER and the SELLER and PARTY refers to either BUYER or SELLER, as the context so requires.

双方指买方和卖方；其中一方指合同上下文提及的买方或卖方。

PERSONNEL means the personnel to be dispatched by SELLER GROUP in connection with the performance of the SUPPLY.

职员指卖方公司为执行完成本合同供货项目派遣的人员。

project means {{项目名称}}

项目：{{项目名称}}

PROJECT LOCATION means where the deliverable of the PROJECT will be finally delpoyed. 

项目地点是指本项目交付物最终被使用的地点。

SELLER GROUP includes the following: 卖方集团包括：

- SELLER and its AFFILIATES; 卖方及其附属公司；

- the participating companies in any joint venture with SELLER providing the SUPPLY; 与卖方合资提供供货的参与公司；

- subcontractors (of any tier); and分包商（任何层级）；和

- the directors, officers, employees, consultants, advisors and agents of any entity listed in this definition; but shall not include any member of BUYER GROUP. 本定义所列任何实体的董事、高管、员工、顾问和代理人；但不包括买方集团的任何成员。

SITE means the delivery location of the GOODS and where the GOODS will be erected, installed, commissioned and tested. 

现场：货物交付地点，也是货物吊装，安装，试车和测试地点。

SUPPLY means the provision of the GOODS and the associated TECHNICAL SERVICE including the provision of the TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION. 

供货是指提供合同货物和相关的技术服务，包括提供技术文件。

TAX or TAXATION means all forms of direct and indirect taxation (including taxes on usage, compensating taxes and "ad valorem" property taxes) and statutory, governmental, international, national, federal, state, provincial, territorial, regional, municipal or local governmental charges, duties (including stamp duties and transfer taxes, customs duties, excise duties, port dues and clearing agent's fees), imposts, brokerage fees, import or export charges and occupation of location charges, contributions, levies, withholdings or liabilities wherever chargeable and of any jurisdiction and whether levied by reference to income, profits, gains, net wealth, asset values, turnover, added value or other measure (including social security contributions and any other payroll taxes and including local authority rates) however imposed (whether by way of a withholding or deduction for or on account of tax or otherwise), and any penalty, fine, surcharge, interest, charges or costs arising in connection therewith;

“税收”或“税款”是指所有形式的直接和间接税收（包括使用税、补偿税和“从价”财产税）以及法定、政府、国际、国家、联邦、州、省、地区、地区、市或地方政府收费，关税（包括印花税和转让税、关税、消费税、港口税和清算代理费）、进口税、经纪费、进出口费和场地占用费、供款、征税、预扣税或任何管辖区的债务，无论是根据收入、利润、收益、净财富、资产价值、营业额征收的，附加值或其他措施（包括社会保障缴款和任何其他工资税，包括地方当局的税率）（无论是通过扣缴或扣除税款或其他方式），以及由此产生的任何罚款、附加费、利息、费用或成本；

Technical Documentation means technical documents to be submitted under the CONTRACT, as specified in the [TA] documents.

技术文件是指根据技术协议的要求由卖方负责提供的技术文件。

Technical Service means the technical services to be performed by SELLER under the CONTRACT, including the services or at SITE/ Project location, as specified in the [TA、TP、TC、TD] documents.

技术服务是指卖方在本合同项下提供的技术服务，包括技术协议中规定的或现场或项目地点提供的服务。
