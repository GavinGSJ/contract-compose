## 履约保函（如有）Performance Bond (if applicable)

卖方向买方提供[履约保函]，合同[履约保函]是卖方履行合同的保证，本合同的履约保函的金额为合同金额的{{履约保函比例}}%。保函有效期直至货物安装调试合格后30天或买方要求的其它期限。履约保函为无条件、不可撤销、见索即付且买方认可的银行保函。The SELLER shall provide the BUYER with a performance bond, which is a guarantee for the SELLER's performance of the contract. The amount of the performance bond in this CONTRACT is {{履约保函比例}}% of the contract amount. The validity period of the guarantee shall be 30 days after the installation and commissioning of the GOODS are qualified, or any other period requested by the BUYER. The performance bond is an guarantee issued by a bank, being unconditional, irrevocable, payable on demand, and recognized by the BUYER.
